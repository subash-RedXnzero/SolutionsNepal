<h2 id="introduction">Introduction</h2>

Nepal uses the <strong>Bikram Sambat (BS)</strong> calendar, which is approximately 56 years ahead of the Gregorian AD calendar. Converting between these two systems is essential for official documents, travel, and everyday planning.

<h2 id="what-is-bs">What is Bikram Sambat?</h2>

Bikram Sambat is the official calendar of Nepal. It is a lunar-solar calendar that typically starts in mid-April. BS months have varying lengths — ranging from 29 to 32 days depending on the year (not a fixed 30/31 split) — so always rely on an official BS calendar rather than assuming a fixed month length. See the official Nepali calendar references via the Nepal government's calendar publications (commonly hosted alongside the <a href="https://www.dop.gov.np" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Department of Printing (dop.gov.np)</a> national calendar notices).

<h2 id="why-convert">Why You Need to Convert</h2>

Many official documents in Nepal use BS dates, while international documents use AD dates. You may need to convert dates for:

- Passport and visa applications
- Academic certificates and transcripts
- Legal documents and contracts
- Travel planning and flight bookings
