<h2 id="introduction">Introduction (Quick Read)</h2>

If you need Nepal-issued documents to be recognized abroad, <strong>MOFA (Ministry of Foreign Affairs)</strong> and the <strong>Department of Consular Services</strong> oversee attestation, authentication, and legalization. The process typically involves online submission (via <strong>NEATS</strong>) for certain steps, followed by in-person verification and authentication at the Department of Consular Services (DoS) in Tripureshwor, Kathmandu, and, if required, embassy legalization at the destination country.

This guide walks you through the entire end-to-end workflow — from document preparation to collection and post-embassy steps — so a complete beginner can navigate the system confidently.

<h2 id="what-is">What is MOFA Document Attestation?</h2>

MOFA attestation is the Nepal government’s official step to authenticate the origin and authenticity of a document so it can be used abroad. Attestation validates that the document comes from a legitimate Nepali issuing authority and that the signatures, stamps, and seals are genuine.

It is <strong>not</strong> itself a visa or immigration permission; it is an essential step in the chain of authentication required by many foreign authorities. The process is carried out through the Department of Consular Services under MOFA, with possible pre-verification steps and NEATS online submission for certain document types.

<h2 id="key-terms">Key Terms You’ll Encounter</h2>

- **Verification** — Checking that a document is genuine and corresponds to the issuing authority’s records.
- **Authentication / Attestation** — MOFA’s official stamping to confirm authenticity for foreign use.
- **Legalization** — Final foreign-government step (embassy or consulate) to approve the document for use in that country.
- **Certification** — A separate process (not the same as MOFA) that may involve notarization or translation; terminology varies by country.
- **NEATS** — The official Nepalese online system used for some MOFA-related processes, managed by the Department of Consular Services.

<h2 id="neats">What is NEATS?</h2>

NEATS is the Nepalese online platform for Department of Consular Services interactions. It handles certain steps of document attestation online (e.g., service requests, appointment management, and status tracking). Not every step in MOFA attestation can be completed online; some stages require physical submission or in-person verification at DoS or embassies.

The NEATS portal is accessible via <a href="https://doc.nepalconsular.gov.np/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">doc.nepalconsular.gov.np</a> and is the starting point for many applicants. Verify the exact steps and capabilities on the official NEATS portal.

<h2 id="who-needs">Who Needs MOFA Attestation?</h2>

- <strong>Students</strong> applying to foreign universities who need diploma, degree, transcript, or enrollment documents recognized by foreign institutions.
- <strong>NRNs and families</strong> sending documents abroad (birth/marriage certificates, police clearance, etc.). Note: Nepal is not an HCCH Apostille member — any apostille happens in the destination country, not at MOFA.
- <strong>Foreign employment applicants</strong> requiring verification of qualifications and background documents.
- <strong>Businesses</strong> exporting documents (e.g., corporate certificates, board resolutions) for international use.
- <strong>Individuals</strong> needing personal documents (birth, marriage, police reports) authenticated for foreign use.
- Any Nepalese document that a foreign authority or embassy requires to be legalized.

<h2 id="documents">What Documents Can Be Attested?</h2>

Categories (examples; always verify with official guidance):

- **Educational documents** — Diplomas, degrees, transcripts, mark sheets, certificates, school/board certificates.
- **Personal documents** — Birth certificates, marriage certificates, name-change documents, police clearance.
- **Legal documents** — Power of attorney, affidavits, court orders, notarial documents.
- **Employment documents** — Experience letters, job confirmations, police clearance for overseas work.
- **Commercial/business documents** — Company registrations, board resolutions, affidavits, partnership agreements.
- **Translations and related papers** — Translations may be required (official or certified) depending on the destination country’s rules.

<h2 id="pre-verification">Do I Need Document Verification Before MOFA?</h2>

Often yes. Many destinations require documents to be verified by the issuing authority (university, government department, or authorized agency) before MOFA authentication. Some documents may move through multiple authorities before reaching MOFA; others may require MOFA authentication after verification. Always confirm with the NEATS portal prompt and the issuing bodies.

<h2 id="process">Complete MOFA Attestation Process (Step-by-Step)</h2>

<h3 id="step-destination">Step 1: Identify the destination requirement</h3>

- Determine the exact document and the destination country’s requirements (university, employer, embassy, immigration authority, or government body).
- Check whether the destination institution requires MOFA authentication, embassy legalization, translation, or other steps.

<h3 id="step-doctype">Step 2: Identify the document type</h3>

Determine whether the document is Educational, Personal, Legal, Employment, or Commercial.

<h3 id="step-preverification">Step 3: Check whether pre-verification is required</h3>

- Some documents require pre-verification by the issuing authority (e.g., university or board) before MOFA processing.
- Others may go straight to MOFA authentication after NEATS submission.

Destination country requirements drive this.

<h3 id="step-original">Step 4: Prepare the original document</h3>

- Ensure you have the original document (and photocopies as required).
- Prepare certified translations if required by the destination.

<h3 id="step-portal">Step 5: Access the official NEATS portal</h3>

- Open <a href="https://doc.nepalconsular.gov.np/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">doc.nepalconsular.gov.np</a>.
- Confirm you are on the official portal (security, HTTPS, official domain).
- Create an account or log in if you already have one, per the current process.

<h3 id="step-login">Step 6: Create an account / log in</h3>

- Register or log in according to the current NEATS flow.
- Use a strong password and record your NEATS credentials securely.

<h3 id="step-service">Step 7: Select the appropriate service</h3>

Choose the service that corresponds to your document type and destination requirements (e.g., “Educational Attestation,” “Police Clearance,” “Birth Certificate Attestation,” etc.).

<h3 id="step-applicant">Step 8: Enter applicant information</h3>

- Enter applicant details as requested (name, date of birth, nationality, passport/ID details if required).
- Ensure data matches the original documents exactly (spellings, dates, etc.).

<h3 id="step-upload">Step 9: Upload documents if required</h3>

- Upload scans or photos of the original documents, copies, translations, and any supporting documents as instructed.
- File formats and size limits will be stated on the NEATS portal.

<h3 id="step-appointment">Step 10: Select appointment/date if required</h3>

- Some attestation types require an appointment at the Department of Consular Services.
- Choose a date and time if the portal offers slots.
- Save the appointment confirmation.

<h3 id="step-review">Step 11: Review and submit</h3>

- Verify all entries for accuracy.
- Confirm that the documents uploaded meet the required quality standards.
- Submit the application and save the confirmation/reference number.

<h3 id="step-pay">Step 12: Pay the official fee</h3>

- Pay via the portal’s accepted payment methods or at the Department of Consular Services if required.
- Retain the payment receipt and reference number.

<h3 id="step-visit">Step 13: Visit the Department of Consular Services (if required)</h3>

- Carry originals and copies of documents, translations, and identification.
- Bring your NEATS confirmation and any appointment letter.
- Be prepared for document verification and potential further steps.

<h3 id="step-authentication">Step 14: Complete authentication</h3>

- DoS staff verify the documents and perform MOFA attestation (authentication stamp).
- The authentication stamp confirms the document’s legitimacy for foreign use.

<h3 id="step-collect">Step 15: Collect the authenticated document</h3>

- The DoS will inform you when your document is ready for collection.
- Collect in person with proper identification and any collection receipts.

<h3 id="step-embassy">Step 16: Complete embassy legalization if required</h3>

- Some destination countries require embassy legalization after MOFA attestation.
- This is done at the destination embassy/consulate or through their official channels.
- Translation, notarization, or further steps may be required according to the destination country.

<h2 id="neats-walkthrough">NEATS Portal: Step-by-Step Guide (Practical)</h2>

1. <strong>Open the official NEATS portal:</strong> <a href="https://doc.nepalconsular.gov.np/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">https://doc.nepalconsular.gov.np/</a>
2. <strong>Registration/login:</strong> create an account or log in per the current flow.
3. <strong>Applicant details:</strong> enter applicant information matching your official documents.
4. <strong>Document details:</strong> enter the document type, issuing authority, and any identifiers.
5. <strong>Upload documents:</strong> upload required original documents, copies, translations, and any pre-verification documentation.
6. <strong>Service selection:</strong> choose the appropriate attestation service (educational, personal, legal, etc.).
7. <strong>Appointment:</strong> if required, select an appointment date/time.
8. <strong>Payment:</strong> complete the payment using the official method.
9. <strong>Submission:</strong> submit and save the reference number.
10. <strong>Status tracking:</strong> use the NEATS status-tracking features to monitor progress.

<h2 id="doc-requirements">Document-Specific Requirements (Practical Approach)</h2>

- **Educational certificates** — University-issued diploma, transcripts, mark sheets, certificate copies, translation if required.
- **Personal documents** — Birth/marriage certificates, police clearance, nationality, and identity documents.
- **Legal documents** — Power of attorney, affidavits, court orders; ensure notarization and seal authenticity when required.
- **Employment documents** — Experience letters, employment contracts.
- **Business documents** — Company registration, board resolutions.

<h2 id="neats-limits">NEATS: Rights and Limitations</h2>

- NEATS handles online submission and appointment management for some document types.
- Some steps (e.g., original document verification, authentication stamping) require in-person visits to the Department of Consular Services.
- Always confirm the official steps for your destination country’s requirements.

<h2 id="embassy">Embassy Legalization After MOFA</h2>

- Some destinations require MOFA attestation followed by embassy legalization.
- The embassy legalization step requires you to present the MOFA-attested document to the foreign embassy or consulate for further verification, translation (if required), and legalization, depending on the country.
- <strong>Apostille note:</strong> Nepal is not a member of the HCCH Apostille Convention, so MOFA does not issue apostilles. Where a destination country uses apostilles, that step happens in the destination country under its own rules — not in Nepal.

<h2 id="fees">Fees and Payment</h2>

- MOFA attestation fees vary by document type and destination country.
- NEATS will indicate applicable MOFA fees and any extra costs (translations, notary, embassy fees, courier, etc.).

> **Fee accuracy:** Do not rely on non-official sources for fees; verify exact amounts in NEATS and at DoS/embassy pages.

<h2 id="appointment">Appointment Booking</h2>

- If the destination country or MOFA requires appointments, NEATS will provide appointment slots.
- If there are no slots, keep checking the NEATS portal for new releases. Do not rely on third-party scheduling services.

<h2 id="timeline">Timeline and Processing Times</h2>

- The MOFA attestation process is multi-stage and can involve multiple agencies (issuing authorities, MOFA DoS, and the embassy/consulate of the destination country).
- Timelines vary by document type, issuing authority, translation needs, and embassy processing times.

Do not quote fixed durations unless official notices specify them.

<h2 id="tracking">Tracking and Status</h2>

- Use NEATS tracking to check the progress of your attestation.
- If there is a status mismatch or delay, contact the DoS through official channels.

<h2 id="rejected">What to Do if the Application Is Rejected or Delayed</h2>

1. Read the rejection reasons carefully on NEATS or DoS communications.
2. Gather required documents to address the reason for rejection.
3. Reapply through NEATS or the DoS portal if allowed, following the official guidance.

<h2 id="children">Children and MOFA Attestation</h2>

- Minor documents often require additional guardians’ information and consent forms.
- Ensure translations and translations’ attestation comply with embassy requirements.

<h2 id="nrn">NRNs and Documents From Abroad</h2>

- NRNs (non-residents) can often use documents, but the approach depends on the destination country's rules.
- Some documents may require further attestation or legalization in addition to MOFA authentication. Nepal is not an HCCH Apostille member, so any apostille step happens in the destination country, not at MOFA.

<h2 id="translation">Translation Requirements</h2>

- Many embassies require official translations (certified translations) of Nepali/English documents.
- MOFA attestation does not automatically fulfill translation requirements for all destinations; check embassy guidelines.

<h2 id="originals">Original vs Copies</h2>

Original documents are typically required for MOFA authentication; copies may be accepted for some steps but not all. NEATS will indicate whether originals are required.

<h2 id="security">Security and Privacy</h2>

- Use the official NEATS portal; protect login credentials and appointment references.
- Do not upload sensitive identifiers to unofficial sites.

7. Schedule an appointment if required
8. Pay MOFA attestation fees through the official portal
9. Attend the Department of Consular Services appointment (if required)
10. Collect authenticated documents
11. Complete embassy legalization (if required) and translations
12. Track status and keep receipts for all steps
