<h2 id="what">What Is Shram Swikriti?</h2>

<strong>Shram Swikriti (श्रम स्वीकृति)</strong> is the labour approval issued by the Department of Foreign Employment (DoFE), under Nepal's Ministry of Labour, Employment and Social Security. It confirms that your employment contract has been reviewed, basic worker protections are in place, and you are cleared to travel for that specific job.

The process runs through <strong>FEIMS</strong> (Foreign Employment Information Management System) at <a href="https://feims.dofe.gov.np">feims.dofe.gov.np</a>. You need it for overseas employment, including first-time departures, returns to a previous employer, Korea's EPS, and Japan's SSW schemes. It is generally not needed for tourism, study, business visits, or family visits.

Nepal and India share an open border, so Nepali citizens can generally live and work in India without a visa or labour permit, apart from restricted sectors such as government or defense jobs. For other countries, Shram Swikriti is mandatory.

<h2 id="documents">Before You Start: Documents You'll Need</h2>

- Passport with more than 90 days of remaining validity (commonly cited threshold — confirm the current minimum in FEIMS or DoFE notices before applying)
- Visa with remaining validity for your destination
- Copy of your employment agreement with the company
- Official email from your employer to the labour-approval office confirming the offer
- Self-declaration letter
- Attestation letter from the Nepali embassy for Gulf-country destinations
- Authorized English translation if the contract or Certificate of Employment is not in English or Arabic

If a licensed manpower agency handles your case, some documents may be submitted through the agency's institutional FEIMS account. Confirm which documents the agency is collecting and submitting.

<h2 id="documents-full">Full Document Checklist</h2>

- **Passport** — Valid passport with the required remaining validity; keep the bio page and copies.
- **Citizenship** — Original and clear front/back copies; name and date of birth must match the passport.
- **Employment contract** — Authenticated or verified as required for the destination and route.
- **Job demand letter** — Verified through FEIMS when recruitment is through an agency.
- **Medical certificate** — From a government-authorized centre and valid for the destination.
- **PDOT certificate** — From an authorized orientation centre for first-time or applicable changing-country/job applicants.
- **Insurance policy** — Foreign-employment life/accident insurance linked through an authorized insurer.
- **PCC and skills documents** — Required for some countries, occupations, and visa routes.
- **Payment records** — Welfare Fund, SSF, insurance, orientation, medical, and official agency receipts.

Additional documents can include educational certificates, technical certificates, embassy letters, translations, or a power of attorney. Follow the exact FEIMS checklist for your destination and employment category.

<h2 id="process">Step-by-Step: Applying for Shram Swikriti Online</h2>

1. <strong>Log in or register on FEIMS.</strong> Go to <a href="https://feims.dofe.gov.np">feims.dofe.gov.np</a> and choose the individual login. Enter your passport number, date of birth, and password. First-time applicants can register when prompted.
2. <strong>Fill in personal and employment details.</strong> Match your passport and employment agreement exactly. Name and passport-number mismatches commonly cause corrections.
3. <strong>Choose a PDOT provider and attend orientation.</strong> Pre-Departure Orientation Training covers destination laws, worker rights, culture, and emergency contacts. It is mandatory for first-time migrant workers and relevant country or job changes.
4. <strong>Pay for and complete the medical exam.</strong> Pay through FEIMS and use a medical centre on DoFE's approved list.
5. <strong>Wait for PDOT and medical results.</strong> Both usually take around 3 working days to appear. Wait until both are complete before continuing.
6. <strong>Pay insurance, Social Security Fund, and Welfare Fund fees.</strong> Use FEIMS's own payment gateway, never a third party.
7. <strong>Scan and upload your documents.</strong> Combine multi-page documents into one file and ensure every name matches.
8. <strong>Submit and track your application.</strong> Confirm an actual Approved result before finalizing travel plans.

<h2 id="fees">Shram Swikriti Fees (2026)</h2>

> **Planning figures as of October 2026 — not an official invoice.** Amounts below are commonly reported in FEIMS flows; verify each figure on the live FEIMS payment screen or <a href="https://dofe.gov.np">dofe.gov.np</a> notices before paying.

- **Foreign Employment Welfare Fund** — Commonly NPR 1,500 for contracts up to 3 years; longer contracts can have a different amount.
- **Social Security Fund (SSF)** — Commonly listed around NPR 2,595 in 2026; confirm the current FEIMS amount.
- **PDOT orientation** — Commonly NPR 700; paid to the authorized training centre.
- **Medical examination** — Often around NPR 1,500–3,000; destination and centre rules differ.
- **Foreign-employment insurance** — Often NPR 1,500+; varies by age, coverage, destination, and contract.
- **Labour approval application** — Generally no separate permit fee; confirm the live FEIMS payment screen.
- **Manpower agency** — Government-capped by country and job type where applicable; verify the cap and demand.

These are planning figures, not an official invoice. Fees, age bands, contract-duration charges, insurance premiums, and SSF requirements can change. Pay through FEIMS or authorized providers and keep every receipt.

<h2 id="insurance">Medical and Foreign-Employment Insurance</h2>

Medical clearance and foreign-employment insurance are separate requirements. Medical confirms fitness for the destination and job; insurance provides life, accident, disability, and related migrant-worker coverage under the policy.

- Use a government-authorized medical centre and destination-specific route where required, such as GAMCA for applicable Gulf processes.
- Check that medical results are uploaded to FEIMS and remain valid through approval.
- Buy insurance from an authorized insurer integrated with FEIMS.
- Match name, passport number, policy number, coverage, and contract period across the insurance certificate and FEIMS.
- Read exclusions and claim instructions before departure.

<h2 id="payments">Welfare Fund and SSF Payments</h2>

The Foreign Employment Welfare Fund and SSF contribution are different payments with different purposes. Welfare Fund contributions support migrant-worker assistance, rescue, repatriation, disability, and family support. SSF contributions support the contribution-based social-security system.

- Use the FEIMS payment system or an official integrated channel.
- Keep the transaction ID and receipt for every payment.
- Confirm the payment status changes inside FEIMS before submitting.
- Do not pay government contributions into an agent's personal bank account.

<h2 id="status">How to Check Your Shram Swikriti Status</h2>

DoFE runs status checking as a separate tool at <strong>foreignjob.dofe.gov.np</strong>, under Passport Search.

1. Go to foreignjob.dofe.gov.np and select Passport Search.
2. Enter your passport number.
3. Submit the search.
4. Review the result and confirm the employer, country, and other details.
5. Save or print the result if that option is available.

- **Pending / under process** — Application or payment received; review is not finished. Wait and check periodically.
- **Approved** — Labour approval issued. Confirm employer, country, and validity before travel.
- **Returned for correction** — Fix the identified issue and resubmit through the same application.
- **Rejected** — Contact DoFE labour approval before reapplying.
- **No record found** — Check the passport number and allow time if you just applied.

> **Travel only after approval:** A Pending status is not a guarantee. Hold off on finalizing travel until the status genuinely shows Approved.

<h2 id="reentry">Re-Entry and Renewal</h2>

A returning worker who is going back to the same employer and country after home leave may use the re-entry or re-approval route in FEIMS when eligible. Changing employer, job, or destination normally requires a new labour-approval application.

1. Log in to the existing FEIMS account used for the previous approval.
2. Choose Re-Entry, Re-Approval, or Renewal as shown by the current portal.
3. Enter the previous approval reference and current passport, visa, employer, and contract details.
4. Upload current employment, visa, insurance, and other requested documents.
5. Complete any current Welfare Fund, SSF, or insurance payment requirement.
6. Submit and download the approved re-entry labour permit.

Requirements can differ by destination and policy. A previous approval that has expired, a changed employer, or a changed occupation can move the case back to the new-approval route.

<h2 id="visa-distinction">Shram Swikriti vs Visa and Work Permit</h2>

- **Shram Swikriti** — Nepal DoFE labour approval authorizing departure for the specific foreign job.
- **Work visa or employment visa** — Destination-country permission to enter and work.
- **Employment contract** — Agreement defining employer, job, salary, duration, and conditions.
- **Insurance** — Migrant-worker life and accident cover linked through the approved route.
- **PDOT certificate** — Proof of pre-departure orientation for applicable workers.

> **You need both sides:** A destination work visa does not automatically complete Nepal's labour-approval requirement. Do not depart for foreign employment until the FEIMS status is Approved.

<h2 id="scams">How to Avoid Foreign-Employment Scams</h2>

- Verify the manpower agency is licensed by DoFE.
- Check that the job demand appears in the official Foreign Employment Search or FEIMS route.
- Compare the employment contract with the job, salary, hours, and benefits promised verbally.
- Pay government charges through official channels and demand receipts for agency payments.
- Never surrender your passport to an unauthorized intermediary or employer.
- Never share FEIMS passwords, OTPs, or account-recovery codes.
- Avoid tourist-visa work promises, personal-account payments, and unofficial FEIMS websites.
- Save DoFE call-centre 1140 (toll-free as listed on dofe.gov.np, October 2026; some sources cite 1141 — confirm before calling, and do not confuse with DoNIDCR's 1147) and the Nepali embassy contact for your destination.

<h2 id="renewals">A Note on Renewals</h2>

For a return to the same employer and country after home leave, DoFE introduced an automatic-approval arrangement in FEIMS for qualifying same-country, same-company cases — see the DoFE notice "FEIMS प्रणालीमा सोही मुलुक तथा सोही कम्पनीमा पुनः जाने सेवाग्राहीका लागि स्वचालित स्वीकृति व्यवस्था" dated 18 Jestha 2083 (June 2026) at <a href="https://dofe.gov.np/content/243/automatic-approval-system-for-customers-returning-to/">dofe.gov.np</a>. Reported conditions include valid passport, visa and contract with current insurance and fund contributions (confirm the exact eligibility in the notice and live FEIMS flow before relying on it). If the employer or destination changes, the shortcut does not apply and you need fresh employer documents.

<h2 id="mistakes">Common Mistakes First-Time Applicants Make</h2>

- Using the institutional login instead of the individual login
- Letting passport or visa validity approach the 90-day minimum
- Mismatched names across passport, contract, and other documents
- Choosing a PDOT class date or medical centre that conflicts with travel plans
- Using a medical centre outside DoFE's approved list
- Attempting payment before both PDOT and medical results are confirmed
- Uploading blurry, cropped, or split-up document scans
- Paying anyone outside FEIMS's own payment gateway

<h2 id="verify">Verify Before You Travel</h2>

- **Foreign Employment Search** — Verify a job posting or offer — foreignjob.dofe.gov.np
- **FEIMS** — Apply for, pay for, and manage Shram Swikriti — feims.dofe.gov.np
- **Labour Approval Search** — Check final approval status — foreignjob.dofe.gov.np

If a job cannot be found through Foreign Employment Search, or someone asks you to skip FEIMS and pay them directly, treat it as a red flag and verify with DoFE before handing over money.
