<h2 id="introduction">Introduction</h2>

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a simple calculation using your height and weight. It helps determine whether you're at a healthy weight.

<h2 id="formula">BMI Formula</h2>

```text
BMI = Weight (kg) / Height (m)²
```

<h2 id="categories">BMI Categories</h2>

- **Underweight** — Below 18.5
- **Normal** — 18.5 – 24.9
- **Overweight** — 25.0 – 29.9
- **Obese** — 30.0 and above

<h2 id="conclusion">Conclusion</h2>

Use the formula above to calculate your BMI instantly from your height and weight. The cutoffs above follow the <a href="https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/obesity-and-overweight" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">World Health Organization (WHO) BMI classification</a> for adults.
