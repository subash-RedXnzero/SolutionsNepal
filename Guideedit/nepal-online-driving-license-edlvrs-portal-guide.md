<h2 id="quick-answer">Quick Answer: How EDLVRS Works</h2>

In 2026, Nepal's online driving-license process generally follows this sequence: <strong>register online → complete the form → select a transport office and appointment → pay the fee → attend verification and biometrics → take the written exam and practical trial where required → wait for printing → collect or download the available license proof</strong>.

The process is not fully online. EDLVRS moves registration, application, payment, appointment booking, and tracking online, but identity verification, fingerprints, photographs, written exams, medical checks, and trials still require physical attendance when applicable.

> **Check before applying:** Portals, quotas, fees, category rules, and appointment procedures can change. Confirm the current instructions on your province's official .gov.np portal and DoTM notices.

<h2 id="what-is">What Is EDLVRS in Nepal?</h2>

<strong>EDLVRS</strong> stands for Electronic Driving License and Vehicle Registration System. It is the online system used for driving-license applications, renewals, category additions, related payments, appointments, and application tracking.

- Applicants register and submit license requests online.
- Provincial portals manage office selection, appointment capacity, and payment flows.
- NID and Nagarik App integrations can reduce manual data entry where enabled.
- Transport offices still perform physical verification, biometrics, examinations, and trials.

DoTM sets national policy and standards, while provincial systems and transport offices manage the local application workflow. You normally use the portal for the province where your chosen transport office is located, which may not be your home province.

<h2 id="systems">EDLVRS vs Nagarik App vs Physical License</h2>

- **EDLVRS** — The dedicated web portal for driving-license applications, renewals, category additions, payments, appointments, and tracking.
- **Nagarik App** — A broader government-services app that can display digital records and provide QR identity data where integrated; it is not the main license-application portal.
- **Physical smart license** — The plastic card issued after the required application, verification, exams, trial, and printing steps. It remains important where an office or checkpoint requires it.

<h2 id="eligibility">Who Can Apply Online?</h2>

EDLVRS can support new licenses, category additions, renewals, and duplicate requests where the selected provincial portal offers those services.

- **Category A** — Motorcycles and scooters up to the applicable engine limit; minimum age is commonly 16.
- **Category A heavy / higher capacity** — Higher-capacity two-wheelers may have a minimum age of 18 and additional conditions.
- **Category B** — Private cars, jeeps, and pick-ups within the applicable weight limit; minimum age is commonly 18.
- **Categories C and D** — Light trucks, tempos, heavy vehicles, buses, and similar vehicles; age and prior-category requirements vary.
- **Specialized categories** — Tractors, minibuses, cranes, excavators, and other categories can have different age and experience rules.

Category definitions and age limits can be revised. Treat the current portal eligibility screen and official DoTM or provincial notice as controlling, especially for heavy and specialized vehicles.

<h2 id="register">How to Register for EDLVRS</h2>

1. <strong>Open the correct provincial portal.</strong> Choose the province where your preferred transport office is located.
2. <strong>Create an account.</strong> Select Register or Sign Up and enter a mobile number registered in your own name.
3. <strong>Verify the mobile number.</strong> Enter the OTP sent by SMS. If it does not arrive, check signal, SIM activity, and the number you entered.
4. <strong>Verify identity.</strong> Use NID, Nagarik App QR, or manual details, depending on the portal.
5. <strong>Complete the profile.</strong> Review your name, citizenship or NIN, date of birth, address, and other auto-filled information.
6. <strong>Set credentials.</strong> Create a password or PIN if the portal requires one, then save your login and application details securely.

<h2 id="qr">How to Use NID or Nagarik App QR</h2>

QR verification can auto-fill identity details from an integrated government record and reduce manual typing errors. It does not merge EDLVRS, NID, and Nagarik App into one system: they remain separate systems connected through supported verification flows.

1. Open Nagarik App and navigate to your profile or National ID section.
2. Display the current QR code on your phone at high brightness.
3. On EDLVRS, select the NID or QR scan option and allow camera access.
4. Hold the QR fully inside the camera frame in good light until the portal reads it.
5. Review all fetched details before continuing; correct source-record errors through the responsible authority.

If scanning fails, increase brightness, use a current Chrome browser, check camera permissions, steady the camera, refresh the session, or use NIN/manual verification if the portal offers it. Never send your QR or OTP to an unofficial agent.

<h2 id="new-license">How to Apply for a New Driving License</h2>

1. Log in to your provincial EDLVRS portal using your mobile, OTP, password, or supported NID/Nagarik QR flow.
2. Select New Application or the new driving-license option.
3. Choose the vehicle category and verify that you meet its age and eligibility rules.
4. Select the province and transport office where you will complete the physical steps.
5. Choose an available biometrics appointment if the portal provides a calendar.
6. Complete or confirm your personal details and upload documents requested by the portal.
7. Review the application carefully and submit it.
8. Pay through the integrated payment method and save the success screen, application ID, and receipt.
9. Attend the transport office for document verification, biometrics, medical checks, written exam, and trial as scheduled.

Daily appointment quotas can fill quickly. If no slot appears, check again later, monitor official notices, or consider another permitted office rather than paying an unofficial intermediary.

<h2 id="category">How to Add a Driving License Category</h2>

1. Log in and select Add Category or the equivalent option.
2. Enter your existing license number and verify the current category.
3. Choose the new category and confirm its age, experience, and document requirements.
4. Complete the online form and pay the category-addition fee shown by the portal.
5. Attend the office for biometrics and any required written exam or practical trial.
6. After passing, wait for the license to be reprinted with the added category.

Category additions can have different exam exemptions and document rules from a first-time application. Follow the exact instructions for your category and province.

<h2 id="renewal">How to Renew a Driving License Online</h2>

1. Select Renew License in EDLVRS.
2. Enter your existing license details and verify your identity.
3. Check the expiry date, category, late-fee status, and any required medical certificate.
4. Submit the renewal form and pay the fee shown by the portal.
5. Book or attend the required office visit for biometrics and medical verification.
6. Track the renewed card through the portal and follow the collection or download instructions.

Renewal may be available before or after expiry, but late fees and grace periods vary under current rules. Do not rely on an old grace-period number; confirm the amount and deadline shown by your provincial portal.

<h2 id="payment">EDLVRS Payment and Fees</h2>

Payment is generally required during application submission or at the relevant stage for a new license, category addition, renewal, or duplicate request. Commonly supported methods include ConnectIPS, eSewa, Khalti, and participating internet-banking gateways.

- **New Category A** — Often around Rs 3,000 in 2026, but province and category rules can differ.
- **New Category B** — Often around Rs 4,000–5,000 depending on province.
- **Heavy categories** — Often around Rs 6,000–7,000 depending on province and category.
- **Category addition** — Commonly around Rs 500–2,000 plus any applicable card or provincial charges.
- **Renewal** — Varies by category, province, expiry delay, and late penalties.

> **Use the portal fee:** These are indicative ranges, not a bill. The fee displayed by the official provincial EDLVRS payment screen is the amount to follow.

After paying, confirm that the application status changed to paid or submitted. If money was deducted but the status is unchanged, wait briefly, refresh the tracking page, and contact the provincial office or payment provider with the receipt instead of paying again immediately.

<h2 id="after-application">What Happens After Online Application?</h2>

- **New license** — Online registration and payment, then office verification, biometrics, medical check where required, written exam, trial, printing, and collection.
- **Category addition** — Online category request and payment, then office biometrics and the required exam or trial before reprinting.
- **Renewal** — Online renewal and payment, then biometrics and medical verification where required before printing.

The exact order can differ by province and applicant type. Keep your application ID, appointment confirmation, payment receipt, and registered mobile number available throughout the process.

<h2 id="biometrics">Driving License Biometrics</h2>

Biometrics normally include fingerprints, a photograph, and a signature. They are captured at the transport office selected during application or at the office assigned by the portal.

- Carry the application confirmation and payment receipt in print or on your phone.
- Bring original Citizenship or NID and copies where requested.
- Bring an existing license for renewal or category addition.
- Bring a medical or fitness certificate and blood-group certificate if the portal or office requires them.
- Arrive on time; walk-ins may not be accepted when appointments are quota-controlled.

<h2 id="exams">Written Exam and Trial</h2>

Most new applicants and many category additions must complete a written exam covering traffic signs, road rules, safe driving, and basic vehicle knowledge. Renewals often follow a different path unless the office specifically requires an exam.

The practical trial is held at a transport office or designated ground. Two-wheelers commonly test balance, controlled turns, braking, and slow-speed control. Cars commonly test turning, reversing, parking, ramp or hill control, and obstacle navigation. Exact layouts differ by office.

> **Do not assume one route:** A trial route or scoring detail from another district may not match your office. Check the current notice and practice the course used by your selected transport office.

<h2 id="status">How to Check Application and Printing Status</h2>

1. Open the application tracking page on the same provincial EDLVRS portal.
2. Enter your application ID or other requested reference.
3. Verify using the registered mobile number or OTP.
4. Review pending actions, appointment details, written result, trial result, and printing or collection status.
5. Follow the office instructions if the status says documents, payment, or verification is pending.

Printing may be delayed by office workload, smart-card supply, verification, or a provincial backlog. A portal status is more reliable than an informal delivery promise.

<h2 id="troubleshooting">EDLVRS Login and QR Troubleshooting</h2>

- **Cannot log in** — Check the province portal, registered mobile number, password, and reset option.
- **OTP not received** — Check signal and SIM activity, restart the phone, and request a new OTP.
- **QR will not scan** — Increase brightness, allow camera access, use current Chrome, improve lighting, and retry or use manual NIN entry.
- **NID mismatch** — Enter the record exactly as issued and correct the underlying government record if it is wrong.
- **No appointment** — Slots may be full. Check later, monitor notices, or select another permitted office.
- **Payment failed** — Check the gateway and bank status, try an approved method, and keep the transaction reference.
- **Payment completed but status is unchanged** — Refresh after a short delay and contact the office or gateway with proof before paying again.
- **Portal unavailable** — Retry later, clear browser cache, check official notices, and avoid unofficial booking sites.

<h2 id="documents">Documents You May Need</h2>

- **New license** — Citizenship or NID, passport-size photo where requested, medical or fitness certificate, blood-group certificate where required, application confirmation, and payment receipt.
- **Category addition** — Existing driving license, Citizenship or NID, medical certificate where required, application confirmation, and payment receipt.
- **Renewal** — Existing license, Citizenship or NID, current medical certificate where required, application confirmation, and payment receipt.

Requirements vary by province, category, and application type. Use the checklist shown inside your portal and bring originals plus copies when the office requests them.

<h2 id="mistakes">Common EDLVRS Mistakes to Avoid</h2>

- Using an unofficial website instead of the correct provincial .gov.np portal.
- Selecting the wrong province, transport office, vehicle category, or appointment date.
- Entering identity details that do not match Citizenship or NID records.
- Using another person's mobile number for OTP and verification.
- Failing to save the application ID, receipt, appointment confirmation, or payment reference.
- Paying through an unofficial channel or an agent promising a guaranteed slot.
- Assuming payment completes the entire process without biometrics, exams, or a trial.
- Missing the appointment or arriving without original documents.
- Ignoring a data mismatch instead of correcting the underlying telecom or government record.

<h2 id="checklist">Quick EDLVRS Application Checklist</h2>

- Use the official provincial EDLVRS portal ending in .gov.np.
- Confirm the selected transport office and province.
- Keep Citizenship or NID details accurate and consistent.
- Use a mobile number registered in your own name.
- Confirm category and minimum-age eligibility.
- Complete the online form and upload the requested documents.
- Pay through the portal and save the receipt.
- Save your application ID and appointment confirmation.
- Attend biometrics and verification on time with originals and copies.
- Complete written and practical exams where required.
- Track printing or collection status through the portal.

> **Security warning:** Use only official government websites ending in <strong>.gov.np</strong>. Avoid agents or unofficial sites offering to guarantee a slot, accelerate a license, or asking for your OTP, password, citizenship scan, or payment outside the portal.

EDLVRS makes a large part of the driving-license workflow easier to manage online, but it does not remove the physical requirements. Accurate identity records, saved receipts, the correct provincial portal, and attendance at every required office step are what keep the application moving.
