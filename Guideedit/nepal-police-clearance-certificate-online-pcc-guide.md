<h2 id="quick-answer">Quick Answer: How to Apply for Nepal Police PCC Online</h2>

Apply for a Nepal Police Clearance Certificate through the official <strong>Online Police Clearance Registration (OPCR)</strong> portal at <a href="https://opcr.nepalpolice.gov.np">opcr.nepalpolice.gov.np</a>. The basic online application is generally free, and an approved certificate can be downloaded as a digitally signed PDF with a QR code.

The normal sequence is: register or log in → enter identity and passport details → select the purpose → upload documents → submit → check status → download the approved PCC → complete MOFA or embassy attestation only if the destination requires it.

> **PCC is not attestation:** Nepal Police issues the PCC. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs or consular service may attest it separately for international use. Do not pay for attestation unless your destination, institution, or employer requires it.

<h2 id="what-is">What Is a Police Clearance Certificate?</h2>

A <strong>Police Clearance Certificate (PCC)</strong>, also called a police clearance report or police character certificate, is an official Nepal Police document confirming the applicant's criminal-record status in Nepal for the relevant purpose and period.

- Confirms identity details such as name, date of birth, Citizenship, and passport information
- Reports whether the relevant police records show convictions, pending cases, or other reportable issues
- Provides an official certificate for visa, employment, education, immigration, or background-check use

A formal PCC is different from a local police verification or a general police report. Check the exact document name requested by your embassy, employer, university, or immigration authority.

<h2 id="online">Can You Apply for Nepal Police Clearance Online?</h2>

Yes. Nepal Police operates the OPCR portal for online PCC applications. Use the official address <a href="https://opcr.nepalpolice.gov.np">opcr.nepalpolice.gov.np</a> and confirm the domain ends in <strong>nepalpolice.gov.np</strong> before entering personal documents. (Portal reachability could not be independently confirmed at last check, October 2026 — if the page does not load, retry later or check <a href="https://nepalpolice.gov.np">nepalpolice.gov.np</a> for the current link.)

- Do not use unofficial websites that charge to submit a free application.
- Do not share passwords, OTPs, or passport scans with unverified agents.
- A portal redirect or Nagarik App handoff can be normal when both systems are integrated.

<h2 id="eligibility">Who Can Apply?</h2>

- **Nepali citizen in Nepal** — Commonly applies for study, work, visa, foreign employment, PR, immigration, or domestic background checks.
- **Nepali citizen abroad** — Can generally apply online using Citizenship and passport details, then download the approved certificate.
- **Foreign national** — May have a different process or additional requirements; follow the current OPCR category and Nepal Police instructions.
- **Refugee or special category** — Use the applicant category shown by OPCR and provide the documents requested for that category.

Applicants abroad should enter their overseas address accurately and check whether the destination embassy or immigration authority also requires MOFA or consular authentication.

<h2 id="documents">Documents Required for Nepal Police Clearance</h2>

- **Citizenship certificate** — Front and back, clear and full-page scan
- **Passport** — Bio-data page and additional pages if the current portal requests them
- **Passport photograph** — Recent color photo, usually 35mm × 45mm, plain white background, and within the portal file-size limit
- **National ID** — Optional or requested in some identity-verification flows
- **Marriage certificate** — Useful when a surname or personal detail differs after marriage
- **Migration certificate** — May be requested when current or permanent address differs substantially
- **Minor or guardian documents** — Additional birth, guardian, or relationship documents may be required for applicants under 18

File types, resolution, and size limits can change. Follow the upload instructions shown in OPCR instead of relying on an old screenshot or third-party checklist.

- Use clear, readable, uncropped full-page scans.
- Ensure the uploaded passport is current and matches the passport number entered.
- Keep the photograph recent, color, borderless, and compliant with the displayed specification.
- Make sure every document uses the same spelling and date of birth where applicable.

<h2 id="process">Step-by-Step OPCR Application</h2>

1. <strong>Open OPCR.</strong> Go to <a href="https://opcr.nepalpolice.gov.np">opcr.nepalpolice.gov.np</a> and verify the official domain.
2. <strong>Register or log in.</strong> Provide your name, email, mobile number, and password if the current flow asks you to create an account. Verify email or OTP when prompted.
3. <strong>Select your applicant category.</strong> Choose the option matching your location or status, such as Nepali in Nepal or Nepali abroad.
4. <strong>Enter personal information.</strong> Match full name, date of birth, Citizenship number, issuing district, address, email, and phone with your documents.
5. <strong>Enter passport details.</strong> Add passport number, issue date, expiry date, and issuing authority when the PCC is for international use.
6. <strong>Select the purpose.</strong> Choose study, employment, foreign employment, work visa, immigration, PR, or another accurate purpose.
7. <strong>Upload documents.</strong> Add Citizenship, passport pages, photograph, and any supporting documents requested.
8. <strong>Review everything.</strong> Check spelling, numbers, dates, address, email, purpose, and file readability before submitting.
9. <strong>Submit the application.</strong> Save the reference or application number and confirmation message.
10. <strong>Monitor the status.</strong> Return to My Applications or the status page until the application is approved or a correction is requested.

> **Match exactly:** A name, date of birth, Citizenship number, passport number, or address mismatch can delay verification or result in rejection.

<h2 id="nagarik">Nepal Police Clearance Through Nagarik App</h2>

Nagarik App can provide access to the Police Clearance Certificate service through its government-service integrations. The app is an access layer; Nepal Police and OPCR remain the system that processes and issues the PCC.

1. Open the official Nagarik App and verify your profile.
2. Go to Services, Nepal Police, or Police Clearance Certificate.
3. Confirm linked Citizenship, mobile, passport, and photograph details.
4. Complete the PCC purpose and address fields.
5. Submit through the app and save the reference number.
6. Download the approved certificate in the app or follow its link to OPCR.

If Nagarik App redirects you to OPCR for a step, that is expected. You still need to satisfy Nepal Police document and identity-matching requirements.

<h2 id="fee">Nepal Police PCC Fees</h2>

> **Last checked October 2026.** The figures below are commonly reported planning references, not an official schedule.

- **Online PCC application** — Rs 0 / generally free through OPCR or Nagarik App
- **Online PCC download** — Generally free after approval
- **MOFA/consular attestation** — Commonly listed as NPR 500 per document; verify the current fee in the Department of Consular Services notices at <a href="https://nepalconsular.gov.np">nepalconsular.gov.np</a>
- **Embassy legalization** — May have a separate embassy-specific fee
- **Private assistance** — Optional agent fee, not an official PCC application charge

The destination country determines whether attestation or embassy legalization is necessary. Do not confuse an attestation fee with the Nepal Police PCC application fee.

<h2 id="timeline">How Long Does Nepal Police PCC Take?</h2>

> **Commonly reported as of October 2026 — not a guarantee.**

- **Typical straightforward application** — About 2–3 working days
- **Manual verification or mismatch** — May take 5–7 working days or longer
- **Peak visa or employment season** — Processing may be slower because of application volume
- **Public holidays or technical issues** — Can delay status updates and downloads

The 2–3 working day estimate is typical, not a guarantee. Apply before your visa, employment, university, or immigration deadline.

<h2 id="status">How to Check Application Status</h2>

1. Open <a href="https://opcr.nepalpolice.gov.np">opcr.nepalpolice.gov.np</a>.
2. Log in with your registered credentials or use the application reference flow.
3. Open My Applications, View Application Status, or the equivalent menu.
4. Enter the reference number if requested.
5. Read the current status and any correction or verification message.

- **Pending / Processing** — Application is under review.
- **Approved / Completed** — Certificate is ready for download.
- **Rejected** — Read the reason, correct the problem, and resubmit as instructed.
- **On hold / verification required** — Nepal Police may need additional checking or documents.

<h2 id="download">How to Download the Nepal Police Clearance Report</h2>

1. Wait until the status is Approved or Completed.
2. Open My Applications or the certificate download section.
3. Select Download Certificate for the approved application.
4. Save the digitally signed PDF to secure storage.
5. Print it only if the employer, embassy, or institution asks for a physical copy.

The approved PCC commonly includes a digital signature and QR code (commonly reported as of October 2026; confirm the current certificate format in OPCR). Keep the original PDF rather than relying only on a screenshot or compressed image.

<h2 id="verify">How to Verify a Nepal Police Clearance Certificate</h2>

- <strong>QR code:</strong> scan the QR code and confirm that it opens an official Nepal Police verification page with matching details.
- <strong>Reference number:</strong> use the official verification option when the portal provides one.
- <strong>Digital signature:</strong> check the PDF signature where your PDF viewer supports validation.
- <strong>Domain check:</strong> trust only verification links on official Nepal Police domains.

> **Do not edit the PDF:** Changing the downloaded certificate can break its digital signature or make the QR verification inconsistent.

<h2 id="mofa">MOFA Attestation for Nepal Police PCC</h2>

MOFA or consular attestation is a separate authentication step. Nepal Police issues the PCC; the Department of Consular Services under the Ministry of Foreign Affairs may attest it for international use when the destination authority requires that chain.

1. Check the destination embassy, university, employer, or immigration authority requirements first.
2. Download the approved PCC from OPCR.
3. Open the current Department of Consular Services instructions at <a href="https://nepalconsular.gov.np">nepalconsular.gov.np</a> (DoCS also runs dedicated PCC attestation at <a href="https://att.nepalconsular.gov.np">att.nepalconsular.gov.np</a>).
4. Create an account or open the attestation service if the current process requires it.
5. Upload the PCC and supporting documents.
6. Pay the current attestation fee, commonly listed as NPR 500 per document (verify in current DoCS notices).
7. Follow the online or in-person collection instructions shown by the consular service.
8. Complete further embassy legalization only if the destination asks for it.

> **Check the destination:** Not every country requires MOFA attestation, and some require additional embassy legalization. PCC, MOFA attestation, and embassy legalization are separate documents or stages.

<h2 id="abroad">PCC for Nepalis Living Abroad</h2>

Nepalis abroad can generally apply through OPCR without visiting Nepal. Use your Nepali Citizenship, current passport, overseas address, and accessible contact details.

- Enter the passport and Citizenship information exactly as issued.
- Use the current overseas address when the form asks for it.
- Download the approved certificate online.
- Check the Embassy of Nepal in your country for consular or attestation requirements.
- Authorize a trusted person only when an additional in-person step cannot be completed online and the official process permits assistance.

<h2 id="purpose">PCC for Work, Study, Visa, and PR</h2>

- **Foreign employment** — Often required by employers, recruitment agencies, labor, or destination-country visa processes.
- **Study abroad** — Often required for student-visa or university processes; attestation depends on the destination.
- **Work visa** — Commonly required, often with MOFA or embassy authentication.
- **Permanent residency or immigration** — Usually required and may need a defined validity period and authentication chain.
- **Domestic employment** — Some employers request PCC or background verification; MOFA is normally not relevant unless specified.

The destination authority decides the required format, issue date, validity period, translation, attestation, and legalization. Ask the requesting organization before applying.

<h2 id="troubleshooting">Common Problems and Fixes</h2>

- **Name mismatch** — Match the spelling, spacing, and order on Citizenship and passport exactly.
- **Wrong date of birth** — Compare the entered date with both source documents and correct before resubmitting.
- **Incorrect Citizenship number** — Check the number and issuing district against the original certificate.
- **Passport mismatch** — Use the current passport bio page and verify number, issue date, and expiry.
- **Blurry documents** — Re-scan full pages in good light and stay within the portal size limit.
- **Photo rejected** — Use a recent color 35mm × 45mm photo with plain white background and current file specifications.
- **Application pending** — Wait through the normal review period, then contact official OPCR support if it exceeds the expected window.
- **Email not received** — Check spam, confirm the email in your profile, and use the portal status page.
- **Password problem** — Use the official reset flow and confirm you are using the correct email or mobile.
- **Certificate will not download** — Confirm the status is Approved, retry in another browser, and contact support if the PDF remains unavailable.
- **Nagarik identity mismatch** — Match mobile, name, Citizenship, passport, and profile details across both systems.

If rejected, read the exact reason and fix the existing application. Avoid creating multiple applications with conflicting identity details.

<h2 id="mistakes">Common Mistakes to Avoid</h2>

- Using an unofficial PCC website instead of OPCR or the official Nagarik App integration
- Entering a name differently from the passport or Citizenship
- Uploading poor-quality, cropped, or wrong documents
- Using an expired passport or entering the wrong passport number
- Selecting the wrong PCC purpose
- Failing to save the application or reference number
- Assuming PCC and MOFA attestation are the same process
- Assuming every country accepts the same certificate age or authentication chain
- Waiting until the visa or employment deadline is close
- Paying an unofficial agent for a free online application

<h2 id="comparison">Nepal Police PCC vs Nagarik App vs MOFA</h2>

- **Nepal Police / OPCR** — Underlying system that verifies the applicant and issues the Police Clearance Certificate.
- **Nagarik App** — Government-service access point that can integrate with or direct users to PCC services.
- **MOFA / Consular Service** — Separate attestation or authentication step for international use when required.

A simple way to remember the relationship is: <strong>Nepal Police issues → Nagarik App can provide access → MOFA may attest if required</strong>.

- Match name, date of birth, Citizenship, passport, and address details exactly.

- Choose the correct purpose: study, work, foreign employment, PR, immigration, or other.
- Use an accessible email and mobile number.
- Save the application or reference number.
- Check status until Approved or Completed.
- Download and preserve the digitally signed PDF.
- Verify the QR code and digital signature before submitting it elsewhere.
- Complete MOFA or embassy attestation only when the destination requires it.
