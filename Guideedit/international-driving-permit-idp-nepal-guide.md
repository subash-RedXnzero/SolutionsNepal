<h2 id="what">What Is an International Driving Permit?</h2>

An <strong>International Driving Permit (IDP)</strong> is not a separate license and does not replace your national one. It is a multilingual translation document issued under international road-traffic conventions, most relevantly the 1968 Vienna Convention on Road Traffic, to help foreign authorities, rental agencies, and traffic police read your Nepali license details.

> **Carry both:** An IDP must always be carried alongside your original valid Nepali driving license. The IDP is not accepted as a standalone license.

<h2 id="need">Do You Actually Need One?</h2>

If you plan to drive abroad — including renting a self-drive car, borrowing a vehicle, or driving during a business trip — you will generally need an IDP in countries that recognize the system. This includes many destinations across Europe, the Americas, Asia, and the Gulf that follow Vienna or Geneva Convention arrangements.

Requirements differ by destination. Some countries accept a Nepali license directly, while others require a different local permit or translation. If you will use public transport, taxis, or a hired driver and will not drive yourself, an IDP is generally unnecessary.

<h2 id="issuer">A Word on Who Issues the IDP in Nepal — Verify Before You Apply</h2>

Public information about the issuing body is inconsistent. Some sources describe the <strong>Department of Transport Management (DoTM)</strong>, often referencing Ekantakuna or Minbhawan offices in the Kathmandu Valley. Other sources describe an automobile association, under names including the Nepal Automobile Association or Automobile Association of Nepal, as the recognized issuing body under international motoring conventions, with DoTM issuing the underlying national smart-card license.

Because this affects where you go and who you pay, confirm directly before applying:

- Call or visit your nearest DoTM office and ask whether it issues IDPs or can direct you to the authorized body
- Cross-check with the relevant automobile association if DoTM points you there
- Avoid agents, websites, or shops offering unusually cheap or fast IDPs without directing you to an official counter

> **Avoid invalid permits:** Fake or unauthorized IDPs can cause serious problems if you are stopped abroad or asked to present your documents to a rental company.

<h2 id="documents">Documents Typically Required</h2>

- Original valid Nepali smart-card driving license
- Photocopy of your driving license
- Passport photo page and, where applicable, visa page
- Original and photocopy of your citizenship certificate
- Two or three passport-size photographs
- Completed IDP application form from the issuing office
- Air ticket or visa copy for some destinations, if requested as travel proof

Your license should be valid and the IDP can only cover vehicle categories already endorsed on it. It cannot expand what you are legally licensed to drive.

<h2 id="process">Step-by-Step: How to Apply</h2>

1. <strong>Confirm the issuing office</strong> using DoTM and automobile-association verification before you travel to a counter.
2. <strong>Gather your documents</strong> — license, citizenship certificate, passport, photographs, and any travel proof requested.
3. <strong>Visit the office in person</strong> and request the IDP application form.
4. <strong>Fill out the form carefully</strong>, matching your license and passport details exactly.
5. <strong>Submit the documents</strong> for counter verification.
6. <strong>Pay the applicable fee.</strong> Publicly reported figures vary, so confirm the current amount directly at the verified office.
7. <strong>Collect your IDP.</strong> Processing may be same-day or take a few working days depending on the office workload; ask when applying.

<h2 id="validity">Validity</h2>

A Nepal-issued IDP is commonly cited as valid for <strong>one year</strong> from the date of issue, though the exact framework can depend on the international convention and issuing body used for your permit. The original Nepali license must remain valid and must be carried with the IDP.

<h2 id="checklist">Before You Travel: A Quick Checklist</h2>

- Obtain the IDP from a verified official source, not an unauthorized agent or third-party website
- Carry your original Nepali driving license alongside the IDP
- Confirm both documents remain valid for the full trip
- Check your destination country's rules for foreign drivers, insurance, and rental vehicles
- Confirm the rental company's own requirements in advance, since it may request extra proof
- Verify the current process, fees, and issuing office via the <a href="https://dotm.gov.np" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Department of Transport Management (dotm.gov.np)</a> before visiting any counter

> **Verify before applying:** This article is for general informational purposes. Public sources disagree on some procedural details, particularly which body issues the IDP. Confirm the current process, fees, and requirements directly with DoTM or the relevant automobile association before applying.
