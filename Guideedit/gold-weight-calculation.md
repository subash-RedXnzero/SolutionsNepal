<h2 id="introduction">Introduction</h2>

Gold is measured differently in Nepal than in the rest of the world. Understanding the local units helps you get the best deals.

<h2 id="units">Gold Measurement Units</h2>

- <strong>Tola</strong> — 1 tola = 11.664 grams (standard in Nepal)
- <strong>Lal</strong> — 1 tola = 100 lal, so 1 lal ≈ 0.11664 grams
- <strong>Gram</strong> — international standard unit

> **Note:** Older or informal breakdowns sometimes describe a tola in pawa/kori terms, but the standard, verifiable subdivision used across Nepal's gold market (and in our companion hallmark guide) is **1 tola = 100 lal**. Stick to tola–lal–gram conversions to avoid confusion at the shop.

<h2 id="price">How Gold Price Works</h2>

Gold prices in Nepal are published daily by FENEGOSIDA (Federation of Nepal Gold & Silver Dealers' Association). The published rate is derived from international spot prices converted at the Nepal Rastra Bank USD/NPR reference rate, plus import duty and applicable taxes — it is the base metal rate per tola (adjusted for purity: 24K/22K/18K), before making charges, wastage, and shop-level fees.
