<h2 id="introduction">Introduction</h2>

If you need government services in Nepal, <strong>Nagarik App</strong> is the official mobile platform designed to give citizens access to multiple services from one app. You can link the following where available:

- National ID
- Digital Driving License
- PAN/tax services
- vehicle information
- voter-related services.

Services may change as integrations expand.

The basic journey:

1. <strong>Install App</strong>
2. <strong>Register</strong>
3. <strong>Verify Identity</strong>
4. <strong>Link Available Services</strong>
5. <strong>Use Digital Government Services</strong>

This guide explains how to use Nagarik App with practical, step-by-step instructions, focusing on what you can do today, what you need to prepare, and how to resolve common issues.

<h2 id="what-is">What Is Nagarik App in Nepal?</h2>

- <strong>What it is:</strong> Nagarik App is the Government of Nepal’s official digital-service gateway, bringing several public services into a single mobile app.
- <strong>Who operates/supports it:</strong> government agencies and partner service providers coordinate with the app through official channels.
- <strong>Its purpose:</strong> to streamline access to government services, reduce queues, and enable remote verification, payments, and service access.
- <strong>Why citizens use it:</strong> convenience, accessibility, and the ability to view linked records (National ID, driving license, PAN) and pay certain fees digitally.
- <strong>How it connects to government services:</strong> Nagarik App acts as an integration layer that taps into government databases and service providers for eligible features.
- <strong>Difference from individual government websites/apps:</strong> Nagarik App aggregates supported services from multiple departments; some functions remain accessible only through official portals.

<h2 id="services">What Services Are Available on Nagarik App?</h2>

> **Note:** Availability varies by province, service provider, and current integrations. If a service is not available in your area, use the official department portal or your bank’s services.

- **National ID** — Verify/access available National ID information. Availability depends on integration with the NID department; follow in-app prompts.
- **Digital Driving License** — View digital license details and QR code. Availability depends on DoTM integration; follow instructions in-app.
- **PAN** — Access PAN/tax services linked with IRD. Availability depends on integration; some functions may require IRD portals.
- **Vehicle/Bluebook** — View vehicle registration data and Bluebook details. Availability varies by region and data access.
- **Vehicle Tax** — Pay or view due amounts (where supported). Availability varies by province and payment providers.
- **Voter ID** — Access voter-related information (where integrated). Availability depends on Election Commission integration.
- **e-KYC** — Use supported identity verification for banking and services. Availability depends on partner banks and integrations.
- **Nagarik App login** — Centralized login for integrated services. Single sign-on experience may be updated with app versions.

<h2 id="registration">How to Register for Nagarik App</h2>

<h3 id="step-download">Step 1: Download the Official App</h3>

- Use the legitimate app store or official sources.
- Verify the developer/publisher is the government or an authorized entity.
- Avoid fake APKs or third-party sites.

<h3 id="step-mobile">Step 2: Enter Your Mobile Number</h3>

- Start registration with a mobile number you own and can receive SMS/OTP on.
- Follow the prompts; do not share OTPs with others.

<h3 id="step-verify">Step 3: Verify Your Identity</h3>

- Identity verification may involve linking documents (National ID/NID, Driving License, Voter ID) depending on available services.
- Have your documents ready; verification methods vary by service and bank/department integrations.

<h3 id="step-account">Step 4: Create/Login to Your Account</h3>

- Set up a secure PIN or password as directed.
- If you already have an account, log in with the provided credentials.

Screens and verification steps can change with app updates.

<h2 id="national-id">How to Link National ID With Nagarik App</h2>

1. Open Nagarik App and go to the National ID service.
2. Enter required information from your National ID or Citizenship certificate.
3. Complete the verification steps (as prompted).
4. Confirm the linked National ID in the app.
5. Access National ID-related services that are integrated.

<h3 id="national-id-issues">What to do if issues arise</h3>

- **National ID cannot be found** — Double-check ID numbers and district; ensure the ID is registered with the National ID system.
- **Details don’t match** — Re-enter exact data from official records; try again.
- **Verification fails** — Retry after a short wait; ensure your mobile number is the one registered with the IDs; contact Nagarik support if needed.
- **Mobile number differs** — Use the number you registered with the ID records.
- **ID information not updated** — Some records take time to sync; check for updates and retry later.

<h2 id="driving-license">How to Access Your Digital Driving License</h2>

1. Locate the Driving License service in Nagarik App.
2. Link or verify your license data as prompted.
3. View license details in the app; a QR code may be generated for verification where accepted.
4. The digital license may be accepted in some scenarios; follow Department of Transport Management guidance for acceptance.

If the license doesn’t appear, ensure data is correctly linked and refreshed. Contact the issuing authority if needed.

> **Important:** Do not assume digital licenses are universally accepted in all situations. Verify with the DoTM for your use case.

<h2 id="bluebook">How to Link or View Your Vehicle Bluebook</h2>

1. Access the Vehicle/Bluebook service in the app.
2. If data is available, view vehicle registration details and Bluebook information.
3. See owner information and current vehicle status where shown.
4. Renewal and tax information may appear if integrated.

Do not claim Nagarik App fully replaces the physical Bluebook where it is not officially supported.

<h2 id="vehicle-tax">How to Pay Vehicle Tax Through Nagarik App</h2>

If supported in your region:

1. Open Vehicle Tax or Vehicle Service in Nagarik App.
2. Select your vehicle and view the due amount.
3. Choose a payment method (as supported by your region and banks).
4. Complete payment and save the digital receipt.

Digital receipts may appear in-app or in your connected bank app.

> **Important:** Payment availability and methods vary by province and bank. If not available, use the official provincial portal or bank payment options.

<h2 id="pan">How to Access PAN and Tax Services</h2>

- PAN services within Nagarik App depend on IRD integration.
- You may view PAN status or access related taxpayer services where integrated.
- Nagarik App is not the sole portal for IRD services; some functions require IRD’s own platform.
- For filing or detailed tax actions, use the official IRD portal when required and reflect confirmations in Nagarik App if supported.

<h2 id="voter-id">How to Access Voter ID / Election Services</h2>

- Some voter-related services are integrated with the Election Commission via Nagarik App.
- You may view voter information or access certain election-related services as integrated.
- It is not a replacement for a physical voter card in all cases; follow Election Commission guidance for official use.

<h2 id="ekyc">e-KYC Through Nagarik App</h2>

- <strong>What is e-KYC?</strong> Electronic Know Your Customer verification used by banks and some services to verify identity digitally.
- <strong>Availability:</strong> depends on the participating banks and the current integration with Nagarik App.
- <strong>Data usage:</strong> identity details provided can be used to verify identity for services.
- <strong>Privacy/security:</strong> share data only with verified institutions through official Nagarik App workflows.

<h2 id="troubleshooting">Nagarik App Login Problems and Troubleshooting</h2>

- **OTP not received** — Check mobile signal, ensure the SIM is in your name, and retry the OTP.
- **Login not working** — Ensure the app is updated, verify credentials, and check internet connectivity.
- **National ID not found** — Verify ID data with the National ID authority and ensure registration status.
- **Driving license missing** — Confirm license data is linked and refreshed.
- **Vehicle not showing** — Check vehicle registration in the Bluebook service; verify ownership.
- **PAN information missing** — Confirm PAN is active with IRD; verify data in the app.
- **Verification failed** — Review data accuracy, ensure correct mobile number, and retry.
- **App crashing** — Update the app, clear cache, restart device, or reinstall if needed.
- **Service unavailable** — Service maintenance or outages may occur; retry later.

<h2 id="verification-failing">Why Is My Nagarik App Verification Failing?</h2>

Common reasons:

- Incorrect personal details entered.
- Mismatch between government records and app data.
- Mobile number not correctly linked to identity records.
- Government databases not updated yet (temporary).
- Service is temporarily down for maintenance.
- Incomplete or incorrect registration steps.

What to do:

1. Review and correct data, then retry.
2. Ensure you are using the official Nagarik App and latest version.
3. Check service status notices from Nagarik or the relevant department.
4. Contact the appropriate department or Nagarik support if problems persist.

<h2 id="safety">Is Nagarik App Safe?</h2>

- Use only the official Nagarik App from trusted stores.
- Keep the app updated to receive security patches.
- Never share OTPs, passwords, or PINs with anyone.
- Review requested permissions; only grant necessary ones.
- Avoid unofficial APKs or third-party installers.
- Be cautious with screenshots containing sensitive information.
- Report suspicious activity to Nagarik support or the relevant department.

<h2 id="abroad">Can Nepalis Living Abroad Use Nagarik App?</h2>

Usually only when they still have access to a Nepal-registered mobile number that can receive OTPs — foreign phone numbers are generally not supported in the standard registration flow.

- OTP delivery and identity verification can be affected by international roaming and network conditions.
- Check whether your mobile number is registered in Nepal and whether you can receive required verifications.
- Some services may require local verification steps or access to Nepal-based records.

<h2 id="vs-websites">Nagarik App vs Government Websites</h2>

- <strong>Nagarik App:</strong> a centralized gateway aggregating supported services for mobile access.
- <strong>Government websites:</strong> department-specific portals with direct access to a particular service.

Nagarik App provides convenient access for many tasks when supported; some functions still require visiting the official department site.
