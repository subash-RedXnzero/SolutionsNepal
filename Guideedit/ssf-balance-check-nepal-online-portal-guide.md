<h2 id="quick-answer">Quick Answer: How to Check SSF Balance in Nepal</h2>

Check your Social Security Fund contributions and account information through the official <strong>SSF Contributor Portal</strong> at <a href="https://sosys.ssf.gov.np">sosys.ssf.gov.np</a>.

- **SSID** — Your SSF Contributor ID (commonly reported as starting with 2, though formats can vary — confirm yours on your enrollment record or at <a href="https://ssf.gov.np">ssf.gov.np</a>)
- **Credentials** — Username and password from employer enrollment or self-registration
- **Verification** — Registered mobile number or email for OTP when required
- **Web access** — sosys.ssf.gov.np → Contributor Portal
- **Mobile access** — Official Social Security Fund (SSF) Android app

After logging in, you can view monthly contribution history, employer deposits, scheme breakdowns, and downloadable statements. Always begin from <a href="https://ssf.gov.np">ssf.gov.np</a> or type the SOSYS address directly to avoid unofficial sites.

<h2 id="what">What Is the Social Security Fund (SSF) in Nepal?</h2>

The Social Security Fund is Nepal's government-run social security system established under the Contribution-Based Social Security Act, 2074. It records contributions and provides four broad benefit schemes:

- Medical and health treatment
- Accident and disability protection
- Dependent family protection
- Old-age protection and pension

Formal-sector employees are generally enrolled through registered employers. Self-employed, foreign-employment, migrant, and informal-sector workers may use the relevant voluntary or designated enrollment channel.

<h2 id="online">How to Check SSF Balance Online</h2>

1. <strong>Open SOSYS:</strong> visit <a href="https://sosys.ssf.gov.np">sosys.ssf.gov.np</a>.
2. <strong>Select Contributor Login:</strong> this is separate from the Employer Portal.
3. <strong>Enter credentials:</strong> SSID, username, and password.
4. <strong>Open your dashboard:</strong> look for contribution history or account information.
5. <strong>Download a statement:</strong> use Statement, Contribution Statement, Download, or Print where available.

<h2 id="login">SSF Portal Login Nepal</h2>

Your SSID is the contributor identifier provided after enrollment. If your username and password are initially the same, the system may ask you to change the password on first login.

- Use Recover to retrieve a forgotten SSID or username.
- Use Reset Password when only the password is forgotten.
- Ask your employer's HR or payroll team if you enrolled through a company.
- For technical issues, contact <strong>itsupport@ssf.gov.np</strong> (as listed on <a href="https://ssf.gov.np">ssf.gov.np</a>; confirm current support contacts there as of October 2026).

<h2 id="contributions">How to Check SSF Contributions</h2>

For formal employees, employers are commonly reported to file monthly contributions by the 25th day after the month ends — confirm the current filing deadline in the latest SSF notice at <a href="https://ssf.gov.np">ssf.gov.np</a> (as of October 2026), as no dated official citation for the deadline was verified for this guide. After the employer deposits, open Contribution History and verify the employee and employer portions.

- **Employee contribution** — Generally 11% of basic salary
- **Employer contribution** — Generally 20% of basic salary
- **What to verify** — Month, employee portion, employer portion, and scheme allocation
- **Recent deposits** — May take several days to appear after filing

If a month is missing, first ask HR or payroll whether it has been filed. Contributions can appear late, especially when employers file several months together.

<h2 id="statement">SSF Statement Download</h2>

After login, look for Statement, Contribution Statement, Download, or Print. A statement typically includes:

- your SSID
- personal details
- employer
- monthly contribution breakdown by scheme
- total contributions to date

It can generally be saved as a PDF for records, loan, visa, or benefit applications.

- Complete KYC if statement features are restricted.
- Contact <strong>tax@ssf.gov.np</strong> for tax-related statement issues (as listed on <a href="https://ssf.gov.np">ssf.gov.np</a>).
- Contact <strong>itsupport@ssf.gov.np</strong> for technical download problems (as listed on <a href="https://ssf.gov.np">ssf.gov.np</a>).

<h2 id="app">SSF Mobile App</h2>

The official Social Security Fund app provides mobile access to contribution history, account information, personal updates, and notifications. Start from <a href="https://ssf.gov.np/pages/mobile-app">ssf.gov.np</a> and use the Mobile App link rather than downloading an unverified copy.

Use the same SSID, username, and password as the web portal. Availability and features can vary by contributor category and app version.

<h2 id="contributors">SSF for Different Types of Contributors</h2>

- **Formal-sector employees** — Employer enrollment; employee and employer portions appear in Contributor Portal
- **Self-employed contributors** — Voluntary self-employed registration; contributor pays both portions
- **Foreign employment workers** — Enrollment through EPS or designated SSF registration; use SSID to check
- **Informal-sector workers** — Voluntary registration through the informal-sector channel on SOSYS

<h2 id="missing">What If Your SSF Contribution Is Missing?</h2>

<h3 id="recent-missing">Recent Contribution Not Showing</h3>

- Wait several days after the employer deposit (commonly reported as 3–5 days — confirm current timelines at <a href="https://ssf.gov.np">ssf.gov.np</a>).
- Ask HR or payroll whether the month was filed.
- Check whether the employer filing deadline has passed.

<h3 id="incorrect-amount">Incorrect Contribution Amount</h3>

- Compare the portal with your payslip and the employee rate.
- Check the scheme breakdown rather than only the total.
- Raise mismatches with the employer first.

<h3 id="employer-missing">Employer Contribution Missing</h3>

- Ask whether the employer filed late or in bulk.
- Keep payslips and written requests as records.
- Contact <strong>collection@ssf.gov.np</strong> or <strong>reg@ssf.gov.np</strong> if the employer repeatedly fails to deposit (both as listed on <a href="https://ssf.gov.np">ssf.gov.np</a>; confirm current contacts there as of October 2026).

<h3 id="kyc-mismatch">Personal Information Mismatch</h3>

- Review name, citizenship, mobile, email, and bank details in KYC.
- Submit corrections through the portal KYC update form.
- Email <strong>kyc@ssf.gov.np</strong> for unresolved KYC errors (as listed on <a href="https://ssf.gov.np">ssf.gov.np</a>).

<h2 id="frequency">How Often Should You Check Your SSF Account?</h2>

- Within one or two months of starting a new job to confirm enrollment
- Every three to six months to verify regular deposits
- Before loans, visas, or benefit applications
- At least annually for a complete contribution and personal-detail review

Regular checks catch missing deposits, incorrect amounts, employer filing gaps, and KYC errors while they are still easier to correct.

<h2 id="mistakes">Common Mistakes to Avoid</h2>

- Using unofficial SSF websites or APK files
- Entering an incorrect SSID, username, or password
- Assuming a recent payment appears instantly
- Never checking contribution records against payslips
- Ignoring incorrect personal information
- Sharing login credentials or OTPs
- Creating a second account instead of using recovery tools

<h2 id="balance-vs-contribution">SSF Balance vs. SSF Contribution</h2>

<strong>Contribution</strong> means a monthly deposit recorded in your SSF account. <strong>Balance</strong> or account information refers to accumulated amounts shown by the system. SSF records are commonly displayed scheme-by-scheme — medical, accident, dependent, and old age — rather than as one simple balance number.

> **Verify current details:** SSF portals, app features, contribution rates, and support contacts can change. Confirm current information through <a href="https://ssf.gov.np">ssf.gov.np</a> or <a href="https://sosys.ssf.gov.np">sosys.ssf.gov.np</a> before relying on it for an official application.
