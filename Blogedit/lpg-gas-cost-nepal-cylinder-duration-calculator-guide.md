<h2 id="tldr">Quick Answer (TL;DR)</h2>

<strong>Standard cylinder = 14.2 kg</strong> (NOC refill). Price is <strong>set by Nepal Oil Corporation monthly</strong> — check the <strong>latest NOC notice / dealer board</strong>. Duration: <strong>2 burners ×2 hrs/day ≈ 45–60 days</strong>; cost/day = <strong>price ÷ days</strong>. <h2 id="price">Cylinder Price — Who Sets It?</h2>

- <strong>Nepal Oil Corporation (NOC)</strong> — publishes the refinery/depot price monthly; dealer retail adds transport + margin
- <strong>Check before you buy:</strong> NOC notice, dealer board, or your gas shop’s printed rate — do not use a 2025 blog figure
- <strong>Black-market spike:</strong> during shortage, street price exceeds NOC rate — buying from an authorized dealer avoids the premium

<h2 id="how-long">How Long Does 14.2kg Last?</h2>

> **Rough household estimates, not NOC figures:** NOC publishes price and cylinder weight only — duration below is indicative household experience as of October 2026, not an official consumption table. Your stove efficiency, flame level and pot size shift it significantly.

- **2 burners ×1.5 hrs/day (small family)** — ~60–75 days (estimate)
- **2 burners ×2 hrs/day (typical)** — ~45–60 days (estimate)
- **2 burners ×3 hrs/day (large family / 2 meals + tea)** — ~30–40 days
- **3 burners ×2 hrs/day** — ~30–45 days (higher flow)

<h2 id="formula">Formula &amp; Calculator</h2>

```text
Days ≈ (14.2 kg × Burner efficiency) ÷ (Burners × Hours/day × Flow rate)
Cost/day = Cylinder Price ÷ Days
```

> **Illustrative formula only** — efficiency and flow rate vary by stove/regulator; use it for rough planning, not metering.

<h2 id="tips">Save Gas — 5 Tips</h2>

- Use a pressure cooker for daal/meat (cuts burn time substantially — exact savings vary by dish/stove)
- Lid on — open-pot boiling wastes noticeably more gas
- Medium flame (blue tip) — high flame licks the sides, not the pot bottom
- Clean burner ports monthly — yellow flame = incomplete burn
- Check for leak: soapy water at the regulator, not a lighter
