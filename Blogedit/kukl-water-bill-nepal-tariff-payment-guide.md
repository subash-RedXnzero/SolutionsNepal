<h2 id="tldr">Quick Answer (TL;DR)</h2>

Check at <strong>kukl.org.np → Bill Enquiry</strong> or <strong>eSewa/Khalti → KUKL</strong> with your customer ID. Pay the same way or at a <strong>KUKL branch / authorized bank</strong>. Tariff is <strong>slab-based per cubic meter</strong> with a <strong>minimum charge</strong> — confirm the current slab at <strong>kukl.org.np</strong>.

<h2 id="how-tariff">How KUKL Tariff Works</h2>

KUKL (Kathmandu Upatyaka Khanepani Limited) supplies piped water in Kathmandu Valley. The bill has two parts:

- <strong>Water charge:</strong> per cubic meter (1,000 litres) in slabs — lower rate for the first block, higher for larger consumption
- <strong>Minimum/fixed charge:</strong> even at zero/low use, a fixed minimum applies for connection maintenance
- <strong>Sewerage/sanitation charge where applicable:</strong> KUKL lists sanitation/sewer services, but a fixed surcharge percentage could not be verified against a live KUKL tariff sheet as of October 2026 — check the current tariff notice on kukl.org.np; do not apply a blog-quoted % without confirming

> **Check current slab:** KUKL revises slabs periodically via its tariff notice. Do not use a copied table from an old blog — open kukl.org.np → Tariff for the current rates before calculating.

<h2 id="how-check">How to Check Your Bill</h2>

1. Find your <strong>customer ID / connection number</strong> on the paper bill or KUKL SMS.
2. Open <strong>kukl.org.np → Customer Services → Bill Enquiry</strong> and enter the ID, or open <strong>eSewa/Khalti → Khanepani / KUKL</strong> and enter the same ID.
3. Verify <strong>previous vs current reading</strong>, consumption (m³), and the billing period before paying.

<h2 id="how-pay">How to Pay Online</h2>

1. In eSewa/Khalti/connectIPS choose <strong>KUKL / Khanepani</strong>, enter customer ID, confirm the amount, and pay — save the receipt.
2. Or pay at your branch KUKL counter or an authorized bank counter with the bill.

<h2 id="meter-reading">How to Read Your Meter</h2>

Read the black digits (cubic meters) from left to right. Consumption = current reading − previous reading. The red dials show litres and are for testing, not billing.

<h2 id="late-fee">Late Fee &amp; Disconnection</h2>

Unpaid bills attract a late fee and, after repeated default, temporary disconnection. The per-month late percentage and notice period are printed on the bill and at kukl.org.np — pay before the due date shown in the enquiry result.

<h2 id="mistakes">Common Mistakes to Avoid</h2>

- Using an old slab table from a 2024/25 blog instead of the current kukl.org.np notice
- Entering the wrong customer ID (extra space/zero) — copy from the paper bill
- Paying the previous month’s amount when two months are now due — check “total dues” not just “current”
- Ignoring a sudden high reading — report a suspected leak/faulty meter before paying a spike
