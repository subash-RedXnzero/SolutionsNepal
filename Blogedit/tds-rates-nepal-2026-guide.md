<h2 id="tldr">Quick Answer (TL;DR)</h2>

FY 2083/84 (Shrawan 2083–Ashad 2084, effective 16 July 2026): <strong>service with VAT invoice = 1.5% TDS</strong>, <strong>service on PAN-only bill = 15%</strong>, <strong>rent = 10%</strong>, <strong>bank interest to an individual = reported 6% final</strong> (reported rise from 5% — confirm current IRD circular), <strong>dividend = 5% final</strong>, <strong>contract > Rs 50k = 1.5%</strong>. The first two are <strong>adjustable/advance</strong> (claimed in your annual return); interest/dividend are <strong>reported final</strong> (settled at source — confirm circular).

> **FY change (reported — confirm in the current IRD withholding circular / Finance Act 2083 on <a href="https://ird.gov.np" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ird.gov.np</a> before applying):** Individual bank-interest reported 5% → 6% from FY 2083/84 and two new TDS lines reported (insurance-agent commission 20%, ride-share platform 1%). If a guide still quotes 5% for individual interest, it may be describing an earlier FY — verify which FY circular applies to your payment date.

<h2 id="what-is-tds">What Is TDS in Nepal?</h2>

Tax Deducted at Source (srotma kar katti) is the withholding mechanism under the <strong>Income Tax Act 2058</strong> where the payer withholds a % while paying and deposits it to the IRD on the payee’s behalf. It is the payee’s tax — the payer is just the withholding agent.

- <strong>Salary:</strong> employer withholds monthly per the FY 2083/84 unified progressive slab (1% to Rs 10L → 10% to Rs 15L → 20% to Rs 25L → 27% to Rs 40L → 29% above; no separate single/couple slab, as of October 2026 — see our salary-tax guide)
- <strong>Business payments:</strong> rent, service/consultancy, royalty, interest, dividend, commission, contract, carriage/vehicle rent
- <strong>Legal base:</strong> Sec 88 (withholding on payments), Sec 87/88A/89 (non-resident), plus annual Finance Act updates

<h2 id="final-vs-adjustable">Final vs Adjustable (Advance) — The Key Distinction</h2>

- **Final withholding** — Settled at source — no further liability. E.g., bank interest to individual (reported 6% — confirm circular), dividend (5%), windfall gains. Commonly cannot be refunded; excluded from annual return income recalculation — confirm final vs adjustable for your head in the current circular.
- **Adjustable / Advance TDS** — Prepayment — claimed as credit in your annual ITR. E.g., service 1.5%/15%, contract 1.5%, commission. Excess over final tax is refundable via e-TDS credit.

Check the “Final or adjustable” column in the rate card below before deciding whether to file for a refund.

<h2 id="rate-card">Complete TDS Rate Card — FY 2083/84 (Effective 16 July 2026)</h2>

Verify each rate against the current IRD withholding circular on <a href="https://ird.gov.np" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ird.gov.np</a> before applying — Finance Acts revise specific lines each year. Figures below as of October 2026; hedged lines are reported, not independently verified.

- **Bank/FI interest to resident natural person (not in business)** — reported 6% — reported Final (confirm current circular; earlier circulars showed 5%)
- **Bank/FI interest to company / entity** — commonly 15% — commonly Adjustable (confirm circular)
- **Dividend (resident company/partnership)** — 5% — commonly Final (confirm circular)
- **Mutual fund distribution to individual** — commonly 5% — commonly Final (confirm circular)
- **Rent — land/building/fixtures (Sec 88(1) proviso)** — 10% — Adjustable/final per counterparty (10% municipal rent tax if individual-to-individual private residence)
- **Vehicle / carriage rent — VAT-registered provider** — 1.5% — Adjustable (confirm circular)
- **Vehicle / carriage rent — non-VAT provider** — 2.5% — Adjustable (confirm circular)
- **Service/consultancy fee — VAT-registered (VAT invoice)** — 1.5% — Adjustable (Sec 88(1) proviso)
- **Service fee — PAN-only (no VAT invoice)** — 15% — Adjustable
- **Commission — general** — commonly 15% — commonly Adjustable (confirm circular)
- **Insurance-agent commission** — reported 20% — reported new FY 2083/84 line (confirm current circular before applying)
- **Contract / agreement > Rs 50,000** — 1.5% — Adjustable (commonly cited floor — confirm circular)
- **Ride-share driver platform payment** — reported 1% — reported new FY 2083/84 advance line (confirm current circular before applying)
- **Question-setting / answer evaluation** — commonly 15% — commonly Final (confirm circular)
- **Salary** — Unified progressive slab 1/10/20/27/29% — withheld monthly by employer

<h3 id="service-1-5-vs-15">Service Fees: 1.5% vs 15% — VAT Invoice vs PAN Bill</h3>

The payer reads the invoice — that single detail picks the rate:

- **You receive a VAT invoice (VAT-registered provider)** — Withhold 1.5% — the provider will claim it as advance in their annual return and also file monthly VAT returns
- **You receive a PAN-only bill (not VAT-registered)** — Withhold 15% — much higher, because the transaction is outside the VAT chain. Common for small freelancers under Rs 30 lakh.
- **Freelancer tip** — A VAT-registered client withholding 1.5% saves you 13.5 points vs a PAN-only client — one reason to consider voluntary VAT registration as you approach Rs 30 lakh turnover.

Example: Rs 100,000 service fee → VAT provider: Rs 1,500 withheld, Rs 98,500 paid; PAN-only provider: Rs 15,000 withheld, Rs 85,000 paid. Both providers reconcile the withheld amount in their ITR.

<h3 id="rent">Rent: 10% and When Municipality Collects It</h3>

Rent for land/building/fixtures with source in Nepal is 10% withholding under Sec 88(1) Proviso 5. A common confusion:

- <strong>Entity pays rent (company/registered business):</strong> the payer withholds 10% and deposits to IRD via e-TDS (adjustable).
- <strong>Individual pays rent directly for a private residence to another individual:</strong> often no federal TDS — instead a <strong>10% municipal rent tax</strong> is collected at the ward/municipality (check your local Economic Act). The rate looks identical but the collector is different.
- <strong>Vehicle rent:</strong> use 1.5% if the provider is VAT-registered, otherwise 2.5% (carriage).

<h3 id="interest">Interest &amp; Dividend: 6% / 15% / 5%</h3>

- **Interest to individual (savings/FD/bond)** — reported 6% final — do not add to annual return if final treatment confirmed; many 2025 guides still say 5% (earlier-FY rate — check which circular applies to your payment date)
- **Interest to company / cooperative to entity** — commonly 15% adjustable (confirm circular; municipal rent-tax path for individual-to-individual rent is a different head)
- **Dividend** — commonly 5% final — withheld by the distributing company/partnership at distribution (confirm circular)

Because individual bank interest is commonly final, you generally cannot claim a refund even if your total income is below Rs 10 lakh — the reported 6% is gone at source (confirm final treatment in current circular).

<h2 id="who-withholds">Who Must Withhold &amp; When?</h2>

- <strong>Withholding agent:</strong> any resident person (business, entity, or individual carrying on business) making a Sec 88 payment — not just companies.
- <strong>Trigger:</strong> at the time of payment, not invoice date.
- <strong>Threshold for contracts:</strong> 1.5% applies to contract/agreement payments <strong>over Rs 50,000</strong> per contract.
- <strong>PAN requirement:</strong> always obtain the payee’s PAN — PAN-only service billed at 15% cannot be re-rated to 1.5% after payment.

<h2 id="deposit-etds">Deposit, e-TDS Return &amp; Certificate</h2>

- **Deposit withheld tax** — Commonly within 25 days of the end of the Nepali month of payment (via connectIPS/eSewa/IRD revenue portal) — per IRD notice, verify current deadlines on <a href="https://ird.gov.np" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ird.gov.np</a>
- **Issue e-TDS certificate** — To the payee within 15 days of withholding (electronic via IRD portal — needed for credit; per IRD notice — verify)
- **File e-TDS return** — Via <a href="https://taxpayerportal.ird.gov.np" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">taxpayerportal.ird.gov.np</a> → e-TDS; nil return if applicable
- **Payee credit** — Payee claims the TDS credit in annual ITR via the e-TDS ledger; final-withholding types are excluded

<h2 id="example">Two Worked Examples</h2>

<h3 id="example-service">1) Freelancer (PAN-only) bills Rs 80,000 for a design sprint</h3>

- Client withholds 15% = Rs 12,000, pays you Rs 68,000, deposits Rs 12,000 to IRD by the 25th next month, and sends you the e-TDS certificate.
- At year-end you file ITR on total professional income; Rs 12,000 is credited as advance tax paid. If your final tax < total TDS withheld, you get a refund.
- Same work with a VAT invoice: client withholds only Rs 1,200 (1.5%), you receive Rs 78,800 — a Rs 10,800 cash-flow difference the same month.

<h3 id="example-rent">2) Company rents office for Rs 60,000/month</h3>

- Company withholds 10% = Rs 6,000, pays landlord Rs 54,000, deposits Rs 6,000, and issues e-TDS certificate.
- Landlord claims Rs 6,000/month ×12 as credit in annual return if the rent is adjustable; if it was final municipal rent-tax path, no IRD credit applies.

<h2 id="penalties">Late Deposit &amp; Non-Filing Penalties</h2>

- **Withholder fails to withhold/deposit** — Personally liable for the tax + interest on late payment under the interest provisions of the Act (commonly cited as Sec 117 at ~15% p.a. — verify current Finance Act) + late-filing fees
- **e-TDS return not filed** — Fee for non-submission plus interest for non-payment periods (rates per current Finance Act — verify on <a href="https://ird.gov.np" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ird.gov.np</a>; commonly cited ~2.5% p.a. fee and ~15% interest)
- **Windfall/dividend/interest final types** — Generally no refund route — late deposit interest still applies to the withholder

<h2 id="mistakes">Common Mistakes to Avoid</h2>

- Using an earlier-FY 5% for individual bank interest without checking — reported FY 2083/84 figure is 6% final (confirm current circular)
- Applying 1.5% without checking for a valid VAT invoice — PAN-only bills are always 15%
- Treating rent TDS and municipal rent-tax as interchangeable — collector and credit path differ
- Forgetting the commonly cited Rs 50,000 floor for contract TDS vs no floor for pure service charge (confirm circular)
- Not issuing the e-TDS certificate within 15 days (per IRD notice — verify) — payee cannot claim credit without it
- Withholding 10% on a Rs 9 lakh land deal — commonly cited CGT floor is Rs 10 lakh (reported — confirm Finance Act 2083), but service/rent TDS has no such floor
- Missing the commonly cited 25th-of-next-Nepali-month deposit deadline (per IRD notice — verify)

<h2 id="checklist">Withholder &amp; Payee Checklist</h2>

<h3 id="checklist-withholder">If you pay rent/service/interest/dividend</h3>

- Collect payee PAN + verify VAT status (VAT invoice = 1.5%, otherwise 15%)
- Apply correct rate from the FY 2083/84 card above; confirm if reported 6% interest applies (individual final — verify circular)
- Withhold at payment, deposit by 25th next Nepali month (per IRD notice — verify), file e-TDS, issue certificate within 15 days (per IRD notice — verify)
- Keep contract/vehicle-rent distinction: VAT vehicle =1.5%, non-VAT =2.5%

<h3 id="checklist-payee">If you receive a payment with TDS</h3>

- Check whether it is final (commonly interest reported 6% / dividend 5% — no return credit if final confirmed) or adjustable (service/contract — keep the e-TDS certificate); confirm in current circular
- Reconcile all e-TDS credits in your ledger before filing ITR; claim advance credit for 1.5%/15% blocks
- For PAN-only freelancers near Rs 30 lakh, model whether voluntary VAT registration (→1.5% ) beats 15% monthly
