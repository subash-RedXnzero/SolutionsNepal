<h2 id="tldr">Quick Answer (TL;DR)</h2>

<strong>FY 2083/84 uses one unified schedule for all individuals (no separate single/couple slabs, as of October 2026): Rs 10 lakh @1% → next 5L @10% → next 10L @20% → next 15L @27% → rest @29%.</strong> Taxable = <strong>gross − SSF/retirement deductions</strong>. <strong>SSF/pension contributors are exempt from the 1% band</strong> (pension income and sole-proprietorship income also excluded from the 1% band — commonly reported, confirm current IRD notice). Use <a href="/nepal/finance/salary-tax">Salary Tax calculator</a> for exact monthly TDS.

<h2 id="slabs">FY 2083/84 Slabs — Unified Schedule</h2>

- **First Rs 1,000,000 (up to Rs 10 lakh)** — 1% (SST — exempt if SSF/pension contributor; pension income and sole-proprietorship income also excluded from this band)
- **Next Rs 500,000 (Rs 10L–15L)** — 10%
- **Next Rs 1,000,000 (Rs 15L–25L)** — 20%
- **Next Rs 1,500,000 (Rs 25L–40L)** — 27%
- **Above Rs 4,000,000** — 29%
- **No separate couple slab** — the old single (Rs 5L) / couple (Rs 6L) first-band split no longer applies in FY 2083/84 (as of October 2026 — verify Finance Act 2083 on <a href="https://ird.gov.np" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ird.gov.np</a> before filing)

```text
Taxable income = Gross salary − SSF/EPF/CIT − approved insurance/retirement − allowable allowances
Annual tax = Σ (slab amount × slab rate)
Monthly TDS = Annual tax ÷ 12
```

<h2 id="examples">Worked Examples — Rs 800k, 15L, 30L</h2>

All examples use the FY 2083/84 unified schedule (non-exempt person; SSF/pension contributors subtract the 1% band):

- **Rs 800,000** — 800k×1% = Rs 8,000/year ≈ Rs 667/month (SSF/pension contributor: Rs 0)
- **Rs 1,200,000** — 10L×1% (10,000) + 2L×10% (20,000) = Rs 30,000/year ≈ Rs 2,500/month
- **Rs 1,500,000** — 10,000 + 500k×10% (50,000) = Rs 60,000/year ≈ Rs 5,000/month
- **Rs 3,000,000** — 10,000 + 50,000 + 1,000k×20% (200,000) + 500k×27% (135,000) = Rs 395,000/year ≈ Rs 32,917/month

Check with <a href="/nepal/finance/salary-checker">Salary Checker</a> (SSF + TDS + net).

<h2 id="deductions">Deductions &amp; Filing Steps</h2>

1. Total your <strong>gross (basic + allowances + bonus + Dashain)</strong> for Shrawan–Ashad
2. Subtract <strong>SSF 11% / EPF / CIT / approved insurance</strong> within IRD caps → taxable income
3. Apply unified slabs above → annual tax → employer deducts <strong>monthly TDS</strong>
4. File via <strong>taxpayerportal.ird.gov.np</strong> — filing deadlines per current IRD notice: employment-only filers commonly report Poush-end and business filers Chaitra-end cut-offs, but these move by notice — <strong>confirm the current deadline on <a href="https://ird.gov.np" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ird.gov.np</a> before filing</strong> — with SSF + insurance proof

<h2 id="mistakes">Common Mistakes</h2>

- Using the old single/couple slab (Rs 5L/6L first band, 30/36% top rates) — FY 2083/84 is unified (10L @1%, top rate 29%)
- Forgetting Dashain bonus + overtime in gross — under-TDS means interest at filing
- Claiming full insurance/CIT without caps — excess disallowed on assessment
- Ignoring the SSF/pension 1% exemption (plus pension-income and sole-proprietorship exclusions) — exempt persons should not pay the first-band 1%
