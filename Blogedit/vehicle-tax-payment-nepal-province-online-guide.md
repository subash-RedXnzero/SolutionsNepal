<h2 id="tldr">Quick Answer (TL;DR)</h2>

Pay <strong>annual vehicle tax</strong> to your province (not federal). <strong>Deadline: end of Ashad</strong> each fiscal year. Pay online via your province <strong>savari/DoTM portal + eSewa/Khalti/IPS</strong> with <strong>bluebook + active insurance + pollution/Jaanch Pass</strong>. Tax slab depends on <strong>CC (petrol) or kW (EV) + vehicle type + age</strong>.

<h2 id="rates">Province Vehicle Tax Rates — How It Works</h2>

Vehicle tax is set by each <strong>Province Financial Act</strong> (Bagmati, Gandaki, Lumbini, etc.) and collected by <strong>Transport Management Office</strong>. Base determinants: <strong>two-wheeler vs four-wheeler, CC/kW, private vs public, and fuel type</strong>. EV is charged per <strong>motor kW</strong>, not CC.

- Bikes: Rs 3,000 (≤125cc) to 35,000+ (1000cc+) + Rs 300 renewal fee
- Cars (petrol): Rs 22,000 (≤1000cc) to 70,000+ (4000cc+) + Rs 500 renewal fee
- EV: charged by kW bracket — see Bagmati EV slab in the table
- Public/commercial vehicles have separate (lower) or tonnage-based slabs

<h2 id="bagmati-rates">Bagmati Example Rates</h2>

- **Bike ≤125cc / Scooter** — ~Rs 3,000 + Rs 300
- **Bike 126-250cc** — ~Rs 5,500 + Rs 300
- **EV private — four-wheeler (FY 2083/84 kW table, as of October 2026 — confirm gazette):** 10–50 kW ~Rs 5,000 / 51–125 kW ~Rs 15,000 / 126–200 kW ~Rs 20,000 / 201+ kW ~Rs 30,000 + renewal. (Older drafts quoting Rs 13,000/16,000/26,000 kW figures are outdated — use this four-band table.)
- **Car ≤1000cc / 1500cc / 3000cc** — ~Rs 22,000 / 27,000 / 50,000+ + Rs 500

Other provinces follow similar brackets with small variance — check your province Financial Act notice at your savari portal. Use SolutionsNepal’s <a href="/nepal/transport/vehicle-tax">Vehicle Tax Calculator</a> for a precise estimate.

<h2 id="pay-online">How to Pay Online — Step by Step (Bagmati)</h2>

1. Open <strong>your province savari/vehicle-tax portal</strong> (Bagmati commonly via savari.bagmati.gov.np — verify the live domain on dotm.gov.np first) → Vehicle Tax / Bluebook Renewal
2. Enter <strong>vehicle registration number + bluebook number + last tax fiscal year</strong>
3. Verify arrears and total; you need <strong>valid insurance and Jaanch Pass</strong> to proceed
4. Pay via <strong>eSewa / Khalti / ConnectIPS / NPS</strong> → download <strong>e-receipt</strong>
5. Carry receipt + bluebook + insurance + license; update bluebook sticker at the office if required
6. Other provinces: use the vehicle-tax portal linked from your province government / DOTM site (domain patterns such as savari.[province].gov.np change — do not trust a hardcoded domain; verify first).

<h2 id="penalty">Late Fine &amp; Documents Required</h2>

- **Deadline** — By Ashad-end of the FY
- **Late fine (Bagmati stair, as of October 2026)** — 5% first 30 days → 10% to 45 days → 20% till FY end (Ashad) → 32% per year after — plus you cannot pass Jaanch without clearance. Confirm your province's current-year stair; it revises with the Financial Act.
- **Required docs** — Bluebook original, citizenship/License copy, active 3rd-party insurance, last tax receipt, pollution check (4-wheelers)
- **Common block** — Expired insurance or missing Jaanch → portal blocks payment until renewed
