<h2 id="tldr">Quick Answer (TL;DR)</h2>

<strong>SSF = 11% (you) + 20% (employer) = 31% of basic salary.</strong> The scheme-wise allocation (commonly quoted as ~20% provident fund, ~8.33% gratuity, remainder medical/accident/dependent protection) follows the SSF Contribution & Schemes Directive — the exact split moves by directive amendment, so confirm the current directive text on <strong>ssf.gov.np</strong> before payroll (figures here are the commonly cited working split as of October 2026). Enrolled via <strong>employer on ssf.gov.np</strong>, paid monthly. <strong>SST 1% treatment + SSF loans (housing, education) per current SSF notice — confirm eligibility/tenure on ssf.gov.np.</strong>

<h2 id="breakdown">31% Breakdown — Where Your Money Goes</h2>

- **Employee 11% of basic** — commonly described as ~10% provident fund + ~1% social-security pool — confirm the current directive split on ssf.gov.np
- **Employer 20% of basic** — commonly described as ~10% provident fund + ~8.33% gratuity + remainder medical/accident/dependent schemes — confirm the current directive split on ssf.gov.np
- **Total 31% example (Rs 40,000 basic)** — Rs 4,400 + Rs 8,000 = Rs 12,400/month → Rs 148,800/year
- **Base** — Basic salary only (not allowances) — check appointment letter split; inflating allowances to cut basic is non-compliant

```text
Monthly SSF = Basic × 31%
Employee in-hand cut = Basic × 11%
Employer extra cost = Basic × 20%
```

<h2 id="benefits">4 Schemes &amp; What You Get</h2>

- <strong>Medical, health & maternity:</strong> OPD/inpatient + maternity per SSF limits at listed hospitals
- <strong>Accident & disability:</strong> treatment + disability pension/compensation per degree
- <strong>Dependent family protection:</strong> pension to spouse/children on contributor death
- <strong>Old-age protection:</strong> pension or lump-sum (PF + gratuity + interest) at retirement — choice per directive

<h2 id="enroll">Enrollment &amp; Payment — Step by Step</h2>

1. Employer registers company on <strong>ssf.gov.np → employer portal</strong> with PAN + company docs
2. Adds employees (citizenship + appointment + basic salary + bank) → SSF ID generated
3. Monthly deposit by employer before deadline via <strong>IPS / connectIPS / bank</strong> — both shares together
4. Employee checks passbook on <strong>ssf.gov.np → contributor login</strong>; keeps contribution history for loans/claims
5. Leaving job? Transfer to new employer — do not double-register; claim per SSF exit/retirement rule

<h2 id="mistakes">Common Mistakes &amp; Loans</h2>

- **Mistake #1** — Contributing on gross instead of basic (or vice versa) — SSF base is basic salary per Act
- **Mistake #2** — Employer deducts 11% but never deposits 20% — check passbook monthly
- **SSF loans** — Home, hire-purchase & education loans up to contribution-linked limits after the minimum contribution period in the current SSF loan notice — check the live eligibility/tenure/ceiling notice on ssf.gov.np before applying (generic description only; specifics change by notice)
- **Tax tip** — SSF contribution deductible for income tax within IRD limits + 1% SST exempt — keep SSF statements for tax filing
