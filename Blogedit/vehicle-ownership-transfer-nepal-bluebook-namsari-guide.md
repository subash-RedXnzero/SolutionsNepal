<h2 id="tldr">Quick Answer (TL;DR)</h2>

Transfer ownership at the <strong>Transport Management Office where the vehicle is registered</strong> (both buyer + seller with <strong>bluebook + citizenship + sale deed</strong>). <strong>Bagmati flat fee: bike Rs 200, car Rs 500 (+Rs 10 stamp)</strong> — no % transfer tax. The expensive part is any <strong>unpaid road tax + 5%→32% late fines</strong> that must be cleared before file moves. Legally within <strong>15 days (federal Act) / 35 days (Bagmati notice)</strong>; done in <strong>1–3 days</strong> if clean.

> **Check before you pay the seller:** Open the bluebook: last tax renewal stamp + engine/chassis numbers vs the vehicle. Unpaid tax rides with the vehicle, not the person — make clearing it the seller’s written obligation or deduct it from your offer.

<h2 id="what-is">What Is Namsari &amp; Why It Matters</h2>

<strong>Namsari (नामसारी)</strong> is the Transport Management Office act of recording the new owner in the bluebook (vehicle registration certificate). Until stamped, the seller is still the legal owner for tax arrears, accidents, and resale — handing over the book and keys alone transfers nothing.

<h2 id="law">Legal Basis &amp; Deadline</h2>

- <strong>Motor Vehicles and Transport Management Act 2049 (1993), Sec 37</strong> — obliges the owner to apply with the RC + tax receipt within <strong>15 days</strong> of sale/gift/inheritance at the jurisdiction of registration, using <strong>Schedule-12 (Transfer Application)</strong> with sale price, engine/chassis, seller thumbprint and 2 witnesses
- <strong>Bagmati Province Economic Act / notices</strong> reference a <strong>35-day</strong> window for the same office — follow the notice posted at your DPO; treating it as 15 days keeps you safely compliant
- <strong>DoTM / District Transport Office (DPO / Yatayat Byabastha Karyalaya)</strong> — executing office; retains the registration file and issues the new bluebook

<h2 id="fee">Transfer Fee — Flat, Not Percentage</h2>

The widely-quoted “2–5% transfer tax / stamp duty on sale price” for vehicles is <strong>copied from land registration</strong> and is wrong for vehicles.

- **Private motorcycle / scooter** — Rs 200 (Bagmati) + Rs 10 postal stamp on Namsari form
- **Private car / jeep / van / pickup** — Rs 500 + Rs 10 stamp
- **Public motorcycle** — Rs 300 + stamp
- **Public car / jeep / large vehicle** — Rs 800 / up to Rs 15,000 for large public vehicles (by class)
- **Other provinces** — Same flat pattern, slightly different rupee amounts — confirm at your DPO

An agent may charge ~Rs 1,200 (bike) / Rs 2,500 (car) for queue-standing on top of the statutory fee — that is a service charge, not an official saving.

<h2 id="back-tax">The Real Cost: Back Road Tax &amp; Fines</h2>

The transfer file will not move until road tax is current. Annual vehicle tax (Bagmati example FY 2082/83: 126–150cc bike Rs 5,000; car up to 1000cc Rs 22,000 etc.) + late fines is the bill buyers actually inherit:

- **First 30 days after expiry** — 5% of tax
- **31–45 days** — 10% of tax
- **Until fiscal-year end (Ashad)** — 20% of tax
- **Beyond 1 year (each year, up to ~5 years)** — 32% per year

After ~5 consecutive years without renewal, registration may be cancelled. Cleaning the engraved engine/chassis plates so they are readable before inspection also saves a second visit.

<h2 id="documents">Required Documents</h2>

- **Original bluebook (RC)** — Must be surrendered — original only; carries tax stamps + office of registration
- **Buyer & seller citizenship** — Original + copy (passport/visa if foreigner)
- **Sale deed / Gift deed** — Signed by both parties (Bikri Patra / gift letter, notarized advisable); sale price stated
- **Tax clearance receipt** — Road tax paid up to date (receipt or updated tax sheet)
- **Insurance** — Valid third-party insurance copy (mandatory before stamping)
- **Photos + witnesses** — 2–3 passport photos of buyer; 2 witnesses with ID for the Schedule-12 form (thumbprints)
- **If financed** — Bank loan NOC / hypothecation release (original)
- **If by inheritance** — Death certificate + legal heir proof
- **If seller abroad** — Notarised Power of Attorney authenticated at the Nepali Embassy

<h2 id="process">Step-by-Step at the Transport Office</h2>

1. <strong>Verify before you pay the seller:</strong> last tax stamp in the bluebook, engine/chassis vs the vehicle, loan hypothecation note, and make a written agreement that seller clears or deducts back tax/fines.
2. <strong>Fill the Namsari form (Schedule-12)</strong> — seller signature + thumbprint + 2 witnesses; attach citizenship copies + 2–3 buyer photos.
3. <strong>Pay fee + outstanding tax:</strong> at the DPO bank counter (cash/bank/voucher via connectIPS where available); get the payment voucher → exchange at accounts for a receipt.
4. <strong>File assembly:</strong> receipt + form + citizenship copies + insurance + photos into the vehicle’s registration file; biometric verification of buyer + seller where the office applies it.
5. <strong>Inspection:</strong> staff verify engine + chassis numbers against the bluebook (and vehicle condition/emission certificate where required for age/commercial).
6. <strong>Registration:</strong> office pastes buyer’s photo into the bluebook, records the transfer, and the section head certifies and stamps it. Collect the updated bluebook and verify the new owner entry before leaving.

<h2 id="inspection">Vehicle Inspection</h2>

The inspection is physical — bringing the vehicle matters. Clean the chassis/engine plates beforehand so the numbers are readable; the inspector will match them exactly to the bluebook.

<h2 id="after">After Transfer — Insurance &amp; Check</h2>

- Convert the insurance to the buyer’s name (or take a fresh policy) immediately — a claim on the seller’s policy will be argued
- Check the new entry: owner name, address, engine/chassis, and stamp date are correct
- Online tax renewal via VRS/connectIPS exists for annual tax only — Namsari inspection still requires the in-person visit

<h2 id="abroad-loan">Seller Abroad / Loan / Inheritance</h2>

- **Seller abroad** — Needs a properly formal Power of Attorney (not an informal letter) authenticated at the Nepali Embassy; buyer still attends in person
- **Under finance** — Cannot transfer until the bank issues a loan-clearance NOC — settle this before paying the seller privately
- **Inheritance** — Death certificate + heirship proof replace the sale deed; the heir applies as the new owner
- **Embossed plates** — Aluminium RFID plates have been tied to registration/transfer/renewal in DOTM notices (enforcement phased by office/circular) — commonly cited as mandatory rollout from Asoj 2082 with ~Rs 2,500 (bike) / ~Rs 3,200 (car) installation cost. Mandate date and price could not be verified against a live DOTM gazette as of October 2026 — confirm at <a href="https://dotm.gov.np" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">dotm.gov.np</a> / your DPO and budget accordingly if not yet installed

<h2 id="mistakes">Costly Mistakes to Avoid</h2>

- Treating handover of the bluebook as ownership transfer — only the DPO Namsari changes ownership
- Budgeting a “2–5% transfer tax” on sale price (land myth) instead of the flat Rs 200–500
- Paying the seller before checking the bluebook’s last tax stamp — inheriting years of back tax + up to 32%/year fines
- Buying a financed vehicle without a written loan NOC — the hypothecation blocks transfer
- One party absent without a proper Power of Attorney — informal authority is rejected
- Mismatched engine/chassis numbers (unclean plates) or expired insurance at submission

<h2 id="checklist">Buyer &amp; Seller Checklist</h2>

- Open the bluebook: last renewal + office of registration + tax stamps verified
- Confirm no loan hypothecation (or get bank NOC)
- Prepare: bluebook original + citizenship copies + sale/gift deed + 2 witnesses + 3 buyer photos + valid insurance + tax receipts
- Pay: flat Namsari fee (Rs 200/500 + Rs 10) + clear any outstanding tax/fines at the bank counter
- Attend together: DPO file assembly → inspection → biometric (where applied) → collect stamped bluebook
- After: verify new owner entry → insure in buyer’s name before riding/driving
