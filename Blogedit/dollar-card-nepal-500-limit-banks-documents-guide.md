<h2 id="tldr">Quick Answer (TL;DR)</h2>

<strong>USD 500/FY, one card at a time.</strong> Apply at any Class-A bank with <strong>citizenship + PAN (+ passport at most banks)</strong>, load in <strong>NPR at selling rate + issuance Rs 500–1,500 + reload ~1%</strong>, use for <strong>online USD payments only</strong>. Expires/renews yearly — unspent cap does not roll over.

<h2 id="limit">$500 Facility — Rules That Matter</h2>

- **Annual cap** — USD 500 per person per FY across all banks (not per bank)
- **One-card rule** — Only one active prepaid dollar card — close old before opening new bank’s
- **Load** — NPR → USD at bank selling rate on load day + issuance/reload fees
- **Banned uses** — India/Bhutan INR settlement, forex/crypto, betting, NRB restricted imports

<h2 id="banks">Which Bank + Documents + Fees</h2>

- **Fast issuers (3–7 days)** — Nabil, NIC Asia, Global IME, Everest, NMB, Machhapuchhre — all Class-A; pick where you already hold NPR account
- **Documents** — Citizenship + PAN + passport (most banks) + photo + filled forex form + account
- **Typical fees** — Issuance Rs 500–1,500 + annual renewal similar + reload ~0.5–1% + cross-currency markup
- **Top-up** — Via branch/ibanking request; USD credited in 1–2 working days; keep load slips for NRB audit

<h2 id="apply">How to Apply — Step by Step</h2>

1. Open/keep an <strong>NPR account</strong> at your chosen bank with active mobile/ibanking
2. Submit <strong>forex + prepaid card form</strong> with citizenship + PAN + passport + photo
3. Bank verifies <strong>single-card + $500 FY usage</strong> → issues card in 3–7 working days
4. Load NPR → USD (start small, e.g., $50 test) → enable <strong>e-com + 3D Secure</strong> → test $1 payment
5. Track spend vs $500 cap; request <strong>renewal before expiry</strong> — balance refunded to NPR on closure

<h2 id="mistakes">Mistakes That Block Your Card</h2>

- Applying at 2 banks simultaneously — second rejected under one-card rule
- Trying INR payments for India (use INR draft/card channel, not USD prepaid)
- Letting card expire with balance — refund takes weeks; renew early
- Freelancers receiving income: dollar card is for <strong>spending</strong>, not receiving — receive via bank TT + prove income for tax
