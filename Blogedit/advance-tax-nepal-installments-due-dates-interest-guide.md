<h2 id="tldr">Quick Answer (TL;DR)</h2>

If you have <strong>business or investment assessable income</strong> in the income year, pay <strong>advance tax in 3 installments</strong> under <strong>Sec 94 of Income Tax Act 2058</strong>: <strong>40% by Poush end (~mid-Jan), 70% cumulative by Chaitra end (~mid-Apr), 100% by Ashad end (~mid-Jul)</strong>. Each installment is computed from the <strong>estimate/revised estimate</strong> for that period less <strong>already-paid installments + TDS credits to date + deemed withholding + Sec 51 medical credit</strong> (see Act text for the exact factor). Shortfall from the required coverage of actual liability attracts interest under the Act's interest provisions.

> **Employment-only?** No installments — your employer withholds monthly per the slab. Advance tax is for business/investment income.

<h2 id="who-must-pay">Who Must Pay Advance Tax?</h2>

- <strong>Must pay:</strong> any person who has or will have assessable income from <strong>business or investment</strong> in the income year (Sec 94(1)) — companies, firms, freelancers, professionals with business income, rental businesses etc.
- <strong>Not required if:</strong> the installment amount is below the statutory floor in <strong>Sec 94(2)</strong> (commonly cited as Rs 2,000 per installment — verify the current Act text on <a href="https://www.lawcommission.gov.np" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">lawcommission.gov.np</a>). Note: an <strong>Rs 7,500 annual figure</strong> circulates in guides but is not the statutory test — apply only the Sec 94(2) floor unless a current IRD notice says otherwise
- <strong>Employment-only:</strong> handled via employer TDS; annual return filing may still be needed per Sec 95/96 rules
- <strong>Transaction-based (presumptive) taxpayers:</strong> commonly cited Sec 94(11A) 2-installment track (verify current numbering — see below)

<h2 id="three-installments">The 3 Installments (Sec 94)</h2>

- **By end of Poush (~mid-Jan)** — 40% of estimated tax
- **By end of Chaitra (~mid-Apr)** — 70% of estimated tax (cumulative)
- **By end of Ashad (~mid-Jul)** — 100% of estimated tax (cumulative)

Fiscal year in Nepal runs <strong>Shrawan 1 to Ashad end (~16 Jul to 15 Jul)</strong>; month-ends above are Nepali calendar dates for that FY.

<h2 id="how-calc">How Each Installment Is Calculated</h2>

At each date, compute (see Sec 94 text for the exact statutory factor — commonly applied as 90%):

```text
Installment = 90% × Estimated (or Revised) Tax for the year − (Already-paid installments + TDS credits to that date + Deemed withholding under Sec 90(3) + Sec 51 medical credit to that date)
```

“Already-paid” counts everything in the same income year <em>before the installment date</em> (Sec 94 definition). If 70% cumulative was already covered by the first installment + TDS, the second installment may be nil. Confirm the 90% factor against the current Act text on <a href="https://www.lawcommission.gov.np" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">lawcommission.gov.np</a>.

<h3 id="revised-estimate">Revised Estimate (Sec 95)</h3>

If business conditions change, file a <strong>revised estimate</strong> under Sec 95 before the next installment date. All later installments recalculate from the revised figure. Always revise down (or up) honestly — understating the estimate still leaves you liable for interest on the shortfall against the required share of <em>actual</em> final tax under the interest provisions of the Act.

<h3 id="transaction-based">Transaction-Based Taxpayers — 2 Installments</h3>

- **By Poush end** — Tax at prescribed rate on actual transactions up to 20 Poush (~4 Jan)
- **By Ashad end** — Tax on estimated full-year transactions based on actuals to 20 Ashad (~4 Jul) — less the first payment

<h2 id="how-to-pay">How to Pay &amp; File</h2>

1. Estimate the year’s tax (or transaction base) honestly before each date.
2. Log in to <strong>taxpayerportal.ird.gov.np</strong> → Advance/Installment Tax → select FY and installment.
3. Pay via <strong>connectIPS / eSewa / IRD revenue</strong> and keep the voucher/receipt.
4. File the estimate/revised estimate where the portal asks (Sec 95).
5. Keep the e-payment receipt and TDS certificates for the annual return (due within 3 months of FY-end — Ashwin end ~mid-Oct, extendable 3 months with interest on unpaid balance, per IRD notice — verify current deadlines on <a href="https://ird.gov.np" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ird.gov.np</a>).

<h2 id="interest-penalty">Interest &amp; Penalties for Shortfall</h2>

- **Shortfall vs required coverage of actual tax** — Interest under the interest provisions of the Income Tax Act (commonly cited as Sec 117/118 on shortfall from 90% of final assessed tax — verify current Act text) if installments + TDS fell short by Ashad end
- **Late final return** — Interest continues on unpaid balance after Ashwin end; extension does not waive interest (per IRD notice — verify)
- **Rule of thumb** — Ensure the required share (commonly applied as 90%) of final tax is covered by (installments + TDS) by Ashad end — that is the interest-safe line (confirm factor in current Act text)

<h2 id="example">Example: Rs 3 Lakh Estimated Tax</h2>

- **Poush end** — 40% of 3,00,000 = 1,20,000. At 90% factor = 1,08,000 due, less any TDS to that date. Pay Rs 1,08,000 (if no TDS).
- **Chaitra end** — 70% of 3,00,000 = 2,10,000. At 90% = 1,89,000 cumulative. Less already paid (1,08,000) + TDS = balance due.
- **Ashad end** — 100% = 3,00,000. At 90% = 2,70,000 cumulative. Less all earlier installments + TDS + medical credit = final installment amount.

If TDS of Rs 30,000 was credited by Chaitra, the Chaitra installment falls: 1,89,000 − 1,08,000 − 30,000 = Rs 51,000 instead of Rs 81,000. (Illustrative — uses the commonly applied 90% factor; confirm factor in current Act text.)

<h2 id="mistakes">Common Mistakes to Avoid</h2>

- Using FY (Shrawan–Ashad) month-ends vs English calendar dates — IRD uses Nepali month-ends
- Ignoring TDS credits to each date when computing the installment balance
- Not revising the estimate when income drops or spikes — then paying interest on the gap to the required share of actual tax
- Assuming “small business = no advance tax” without checking the Sec 94(2) installment floor against your estimate (Rs 7,500 annual figure is not the statutory test)
- Missing the Ashad end installment and waiting for the annual return — interest has already started accruing

<h2 id="checklist">Checklist</h2>

- Confirm you have business/investment assessable income (or transaction-base track)
- Estimate the year’s tax before each installment date; revise via Sec 95 if needed
- Compute: commonly 90% × estimate less (already paid + TDS + deemed withholding + Sec 51 medical) to that date (confirm factor in Act text)
- Pay by Poush / Chaitra / Ashad end via taxpayerportal.ird.gov.np and keep vouchers + TDS certificates
- Ensure the required share (commonly 90%) of final tax is covered by Ashad end to avoid interest; file the annual return by Ashwin end (per IRD notice — verify)
