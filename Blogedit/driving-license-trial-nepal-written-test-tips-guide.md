<h2 id="tldr">Quick Answer (TL;DR)</h2>

<strong>Likhit pass commonly cited as 40/60 → Bike trial 7 stages → Car trial ramp/eight/traffic.</strong> Apply at <strong>edlvrs.dotm.gov.np</strong> (confirm the current question count/marks/time on the live EDLVRS notice — variants circulate), pay the <strong>per-category application fee commonly cited as ~Rs 500</strong> (voucher via IPS — confirm the live fee at edlvrs.dotm.gov.np), pass the written test, then trial. <strong>One touch = one fail</strong> in most stages. Result same day on EDLVRS/print notice (office-dependent).

<h2 id="written">Stage 1 — Written Test (Likhit)</h2>

- <strong>Bank:</strong> DoTM question bank (traffic signs, rules, general knowledge) — download from edlvrs.dotm.gov.np
- <strong>Exam (verify the live notice):</strong> commonly described as ~60 questions × 1 mark with a 40-mark pass and ~15–30 min on computer, no negative marking — but 20×5-mark / 50-question / 60%-pass variants are also reported. Read the current EDLVRS notice for your province/date; the notice governs.
- <strong>Tip:</strong> Memorize sign shapes/colors + right-of-way + alcohol/helmet laws — 80% of paper is signs + rules
- <strong>Bring:</strong> printed admit card + original citizenship + voucher receipt

<h2 id="bike-trial">Stage 2 — Bike Trial (7 Points)</h2>

- **1. Figure-8 (Aath)** — Keep feet up, engine on, inside the double line — foot/line touch = fail
- **2. Sumy (Width control)** — Wooden plank — stay centered, no fall, steady throttle
- **3. U-turn (Ghumaune)** — Tight U without foot or crossing outer line
- **4. Ukalo (Uphill)** — Stop on slope, restart without rolling back >30cm
- **5. Oralo (Downhill)** — Controlled descent, no engine off, foot brake only
- **6. Rough road (Khurkhure)** — Maintain line over uneven surface without wobble
- **7. Traffic & Road** — Signal, mirror, helmet, indicator discipline + 50m road ride

<h2 id="car-trial">Car / Scooter Trial — What Fails You</h2>

- <strong>Car:</strong> forward-8, reverse-S, side parking, uphill (handbrake) — touching pole/line, stopping short, or curb hit = fail
- <strong>Scooter:</strong> same 7 as bike but auto-gear — examiners watch brake + balance more strictly
- <strong>Top 5 fail reasons:</strong> feet down in Aath (bike), rolling back on uphill, hitting pole, missing indicator, engine stall
- <strong>Practice:</strong> rent the trial track day before — 30 min of Aath practice fixes 60% of fails

<h2 id="retrial">Result, License Printing &amp; Retrial</h2>

- **Result** — Same day on EDLVRS notice board + SMS; pass → biometric → smart card
- **Card print** — 2–8 weeks province-wise; interim paper license + QR valid to drive
- **Retrial** — Fail written → next Likhit call; fail trial → retrial only for failed vehicle class on next published date
- **Fee** — Application commonly cited as ~Rs 500/category; smart card commonly cited as ~Rs 1,500 at printing (province-waived for some) — both could not be verified against a live dotm.gov.np/EDLVRS fee notice as of October 2026; confirm the live fee on edlvrs.dotm.gov.np before paying
