<h2 id="tldr">Quick Answer (TL;DR)</h2>

FY 2083/84 (16 July 2026): NEA domestic single-phase tariff is <strong>progressive</strong> — Rs <strong>0–11 per unit</strong> across 6 blocks (<strong>21–30: Rs 6.50, 31–50: Rs 8.00, 51–250: Rs 9.50, 251+: Rs 11.00</strong>) plus <strong>one service charge</strong> by total units + meter capacity (5A/15A/30A/60A). <strong>5A ≤20 units = Rs 0 energy</strong> (Rs 30 charge only); if &gt;20 units those 20 are billed at <strong>Rs 3.00</strong>. <strong>Household VAT: first 50 units exempt, 5% on energy above 50</strong> (new from Shrawan 1, 2083).

> **Progressive, not flat:** Only the units inside each block use that block’s price. A 75-unit household does not pay Rs 9.50 for all 75 units — only for units 51–75. This is the most common billing mistake.

<h2 id="how-tariff">How NEA Tariff Works (Progressive Slabs)</h2>

Your meter capacity (Ampere on the nameplate) + total monthly units (kWh) decide two things:

- <strong>Energy charge:</strong> added block by block (progressively)
- <strong>Service (minimum) charge:</strong> one fixed charge selected from the row matching your total units and meter capacity (not added per block)
- <strong>VAT:</strong> 5% on the energy charge for consumption above 50 units only (household, from FY 2083/84)

<h3 id="lifeline">5A Lifeline Rule (0–20 Units)</h3>

- **Total ≤20 units on a 5A meter** — Energy = Rs 0 → bill = Rs 30 service charge only (no VAT)
- **Total 21+ units on a 5A meter** — Those first 20 are retroactively Rs 3.00 each (20×3 = Rs 60), then 21–30 at 6.50 etc. progressively
- **Larger meters (15A/30A/60A)** — No lifeline — first 20 are always Rs 4.00 / 5.00 / 6.00 respectively

<h3 id="vat">VAT Rule — 5% Only Above 50 Units (FY 2083/84 New)</h3>

- **0–50 units (household)** — VAT-exempt — no VAT at all
- **51+ units** — 5% concessional VAT on the energy charge for units above 50 only (not on service charge)
- **Non-household** — Separate VAT treatment — verify the invoice category; do not use the household rule for commercial/industrial

<h2 id="how-calculate">How to Calculate Your Bill (Progressive Method)</h2>

```text
Bill = (Block1 units × Block1 rate) + (Block2 × rate) + ... + One Service Charge (total-use row) + 5% VAT on energy above 50 units
```

Do not multiply total units by the highest block rate — that overstates the bill.

<h2 id="examples">Worked Examples (5A Domestic)</h2>

- **20 units (≤20 lifeline)** — Energy Rs 0 + Service Rs 30 = Rs 30 (no VAT)
- **30 units** — 20×3.00 (=60) + 10×6.50 (=65) = Rs 125 energy + Rs 50 service = ~Rs 175 (≤50 → no VAT)
- **50 units** — 20×3.00 (=60) + 10×6.50 (=65) + 20×8.00 (=160) = Rs 285 energy + Rs 50 service (5A, 21–50 row) = Rs 335 (≤50 → no VAT)
- **100 units** — 285 (first 50) + 50×9.50 (=475) = Rs 760 energy + Rs 75 service = Rs 835 + 5% VAT on energy above 50 (~Rs 24) ≈ Rs 859
- **200 units** — ~Rs 1,790 energy + Rs 100 service + ~Rs 71 VAT (above 50) ≈ Rs 1,961

Note: 50-unit math above is the 5A progressive illustration (first 20 at the Rs 3.00 >20-unit rule). A 15A meter's first 20 bill at Rs 4.00 instead — same method, different first block. <h2 id="meter-check">How to Find Your Meter Capacity</h2>

- Check the meter nameplate (sticker on the NEA meter — “5A”, “15A”, “30A” or “60A”)
- Check your NEA invoice — meter capacity is printed near the customer ID
- Domestic single-phase is almost always 5A (standard house) or 15A (larger house); 30A/60A are less common for households

<h2 id="pay-online">How to Check &amp; Pay Your NEA Bill Online</h2>

1. Open the NEA portal, Nagarik App → NEA, or eSewa/Khalti → Electricity/NEA
2. Enter customer ID / SC number from the bill and select the meter/office
3. Verify the reading date, units and amount before paying
4. Keep the digital receipt — check that VAT above 50 units is shown separately where applicable

<h2 id="mistakes">Common Mistakes to Avoid</h2>

- Multiplying total units by the top slab rate (progressive method required)
- Assuming 5A lifeline Rs 0 applies when total >20 (those 20 become Rs 3.00)
- Using the 15A/30A first-block rate for a 5A meter (or vice-versa)
- Adding VAT to the service charge or to the first 50 units (VAT only on energy above 50, household)
- Using a commercial/industrial tariff for a household single-phase meter
- Forgetting that service charge is one amount (total-use row), not per block

<h2 id="checklist">Bill Checklist</h2>

- Meter capacity confirmed (5A/15A/30A/60A)
- Monthly units read from bill or meter
- Progressive energy blocks added separately
- One service charge from the total-use row added
- 5% VAT on energy above 50 units only (household, FY 2083/84)
- Total cross-checked with SolutionsNepal calculator before payment
