<h2 id="tldr">Quick Answer (TL;DR)</h2>

<strong>Gross = basic + allowances + bonus</strong> (before cuts). <strong>Net = Gross − 11% SSF (employee) − TDS − other deductions</strong>. Employer adds <strong>20% SSF</strong> separately. <strong>Taxable = Gross − SSF (11%) − eligible CIT/insurance − allowances</strong>, then taxed via the <strong>FY 2083/84 unified slab (1% to Rs 10L — waived if SSF/pension contributor — then 10%/20%/27%/29%)</strong> and withheld as <strong>TDS</strong>.

<h2 id="anatomy">Anatomy of a Nepali Salary Slip</h2>

<h3 id="gross">Gross Salary</h3>

- **Basic salary** — Often 50–60% of gross; base for SSF 11% calculation
- **Allowances** — House rent, dearness, conveyance, medical, etc. — some partly exempt where the Act allows
- **Bonus / festival** — Dashain bonus (often one month’s basic) + performance bonus — added to gross in the month paid
- **Overtime** — Hours × rate (see our overtime calculator) — added to gross

<h3 id="deductions">Deductions</h3>

- **SSF — employee 11%** — 11% of basic salary (deducted from you); exempts you from the 1% SST on the first Rs 10 lakh unified band (FY 2083/84)
- **SSF — employer 20%** — Not a deduction — employer’s contribution on top of your gross (total 31% to SSF)
- **TDS (income tax)** — Employer’s monthly withholding per slab on taxable income; reported via e-TDS
- **CIT / insurance** — Employee-paid Citizen Investment Trust (CIT) / life insurance where opted — partly deductible up to IRD limits
- **Advance / loan** — Any salary advance or staff loan installment

<h3 id="net">Net / Take-Home</h3>

```text
Net = Gross − SSF (11% of basic) − TDS − CIT/Insurance − Advances
```

<h2 id="ssf-deep">SSF — 11% + 20% = 31%</h2>

The <strong>Social Security Fund</strong> is the contribution-based insurance/retirement scheme (separate from Social Security Allowance, which is a tax-funded welfare benefit). For covered private-sector employees: <strong>employee pays 11% of basic, employer pays 20% of basic</strong> — total 31% of basic goes to SSF each month. Being an SSF contributor <strong>exempts you from the 1% SST</strong> on the first Rs 10 lakh unified band (FY 2083/84, as of October 2026 — pension contributors, pension income and sole-proprietorship income also reported excluded; confirm current IRD notice) — so an SSF-covered employee with annual taxable income under Rs 10 lakh generally has Rs 0 salary TDS.

<h2 id="example">Worked Slip Example (Rs 80,000 Gross)</h2>

Assumptions: basic Rs 48,000 (60% of gross), SSF-covered, no CIT for simplicity. Monthly view under FY 2083/84 unified slabs:

- **Gross** — Rs 80,000 (basic 48,000 + allowances 32,000)
- **Less SSF 11% of basic** — Rs 5,280
- **Taxable (annualised)** — (80,000 − 5,280) ×12 = Rs 8,96,640
- **TDS (monthly, unified slab)** — SSF-covered: Rs 0/month (Rs 8,96,640 is inside the Rs 10L band and the 1% is exempt). Non-SSF same pay: 8,96,640×1% = Rs 8,966/year ≈ Rs 747/month. (Old guides showing ~Rs 6,100–6,800/month used the pre-2083/84 5/6L-band slabs and no longer apply.)
- **Net (SSF-covered)** — Rs 80,000 − 5,280 − 0 ≈ Rs 74,720 + overtime/bonus month varies; non-SSF ≈ Rs 80,000 − 5,280 − 747 ≈ Rs 73,973

<h2 id="bonuses">Bonus, Overtime &amp; Dashain</h2>

- <strong>Dashain bonus:</strong> often 1 month’s basic, taxed as salary income in the month paid (TDS spikes that month)
- <strong>Overtime:</strong> taxable as salary; rate often 1.5× basic-hourly — try our <a href="/nepal/finance/overtime-calculator">Overtime Calculator</a>
- <strong>Arrears/bonus bump:</strong> can push you into the next slab for that month only — normal, not an error

<h2 id="mistakes">Common Mistakes to Avoid</h2>

- Treating gross as take-home (forgot 11% SSF + TDS)
- Thinking the employer's 20% SSF comes out of your salary (it is employer's extra)
- Missing that SSF/pension contributors are exempt from the 1% SST on the first Rs 10 lakh — non-exempt staff pay ~1% on that band (e.g. ~Rs 8,966/year on Rs 8.97L taxable)
- Comparing two offers by gross alone when basic % differs (SSF is only on basic)
- Ignoring that a bonus month has higher TDS (slab applied to higher monthly taxable)

<h2 id="checklist">Checklist</h2>

- Gross split into basic vs allowances confirmed (check offer letter)
- SSF 11% (employee) + 20% (employer) on basic verified; 1% SST exemption applied where SSF/pension-covered (first Rs 10L band)
- Taxable = gross − SSF(11%) − CIT/insurance (if any) − exempt allowance portion
- TDS computed per FY 2083/84 unified slab (1/10/20/27/29%, no single/couple split) and reconciled to IRD e-TDS at year-end
- Net = gross − SSF(11%) − TDS − other deductions; bonus/overtime months checked separately
