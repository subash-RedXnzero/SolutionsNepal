<h2 id="tldr">Quick Answer (TL;DR)</h2>

Renew your bluebook <strong>every fiscal year by Ashad end (mid-July)</strong>. Pay the <strong>provincial vehicle tax + Rs 300 (bike) / Rs 500 (car) renewal charge</strong> + have <strong>valid third-party insurance</strong>. You can pay the tax online via <strong>Nagarik App, eSewa, Khalti or connectIPS</strong>, but you <strong>must still visit the Transport Management Office</strong> with the receipt + insurance + original bluebook for stamping. Late = 5% → 32% penalty.

> **Which gazette governs:** Vehicle (road) tax is provincial — the applicable schedule is the province Economic/Finance Act notice for the fiscal year, posted at your Transport Management Office. A "DoTM FY 2083/84 gazette dated 15 Shrawan 2083" is widely repeated online but vehicle tax is not set by a federal DoTM gazette — treat that citation as unverified and read the provincial schedule paper at the counter (that paper prevails over any website).

<h2 id="what-is">What Is Bluebook Renewal?</h2>

The <strong>bluebook (bill book)</strong> is the vehicle registration certificate issued by the Department of Transport Management (DoTM). Renewal confirms:

- Vehicle tax for the current fiscal year is paid
- Third-party insurance is current
- Your vehicle remains legally compliant on public roads

Without the renewal stamp your registration and bluebook are treated as invalid — you cannot legally ride/drive, renew insurance, or pass a traffic check.

<h2 id="when">When to Renew</h2>

- <strong>Annual deadline:</strong> by Ashad end (mid-July) each fiscal year. The tax year resets on Shrawan 1.
- <strong>No grace period:</strong> fines start day one after expiry.
- <strong>Early renewal</strong> within the fiscal year avoids the jump to 32% once the new fiscal year starts.

<h2 id="documents">Required Documents</h2>

- **Original bluebook** — Vehicle registration certificate — mandatory
- **Previous tax receipt** — Proof of last fiscal year’s payment (get duplicate at the office if lost)
- **Valid third-party insurance** — Certificate must be current — the office rejects without it
- **Owner ID copy** — Citizenship / driving license / passport / voter ID / PAN — one valid copy
- **Payment receipt (if paid online)** — Printed or digital voucher from Nagarik App / eSewa / Khalti / connectIPS

<h2 id="tax-rates-bike">Vehicle Tax Rates — Two-Wheelers (Bagmati FY 2082/83 Reference)</h2>

Tax is by engine displacement (cc). Add the renewal charge and insurance on top.

- **Up to 125 cc** — Rs 3,000 tax + Rs 300 renewal + insurance
- **126 – 150 cc** — Rs 5,000 + Rs 300 + insurance
- **151 – 225 cc** — Rs 6,500 + Rs 300 + insurance
- **226 – 400 cc** — Commonly cited ~Rs 11,000–12,000 + Rs 300 + insurance (variants circulate — confirm provincial sheet)
- **401 – 650 cc** — Commonly cited ~Rs 20,000–25,000 + Rs 300 + insurance (variants circulate — confirm provincial sheet)
- **651 cc and above** — Rs 35,000 + Rs 300 + insurance

Other provinces use the same cc brackets but rates vary by 2–10%. Confirm at your district Yatayat Karyalaya.

<h2 id="tax-rates-car">Vehicle Tax Rates — Private Cars / Jeeps / Vans</h2>

- **Up to 1000 cc** — Rs 22,000 + Rs 500 renewal
- **1001 – 1500 cc** — Rs 25,000 + Rs 500
- **1501 – 2000 cc** — Rs 27,000 + Rs 500
- **2001 – 2500 cc** — Rs 37,000 + Rs 500
- **2501 – 3000 cc** — Rs 50,000 + Rs 500
- **3001 – 3500 cc** — Rs 65,000 + Rs 500
- **3501 cc and above** — Rs 70,000 + Rs 500

<h2 id="ev-rates">Electric Vehicle Rates</h2>

EV tax is by motor power (kW), not cc, and is substantially lower:

- **Two-wheeler watt bands** — Published in watts (W); figures circulate but unit errors are common online — read the gazetted provincial sheet at your office rather than a blog table
- **Four-wheeler: 10–50kW / 51–125kW** — ~Rs 5,000 / 15,000 (working FY 2083/84 figures as of October 2026 — confirm gazette)
- **Four-wheeler: 126–200kW / 201+kW** — ~Rs 20,000 / 30,000 (working figures — confirm gazette)

> **Commercial vehicles:** Buses, trucks, pickups are assessed by GVW and age, with separate sheets for goods vs passenger. Public-transport vehicles with a current route permit + fitness certificate get the concession amount listed in the commercial table.

<h2 id="renewal-charge">Renewal Charge &amp; Insurance</h2>

- **Renewal charge** — Rs 300 (two-wheelers) / Rs 500 (private four-wheelers) per fiscal year
- **Third-party insurance (bike)** — Premiums vary by insurer schedule year (older schedules quoted ~Rs 1,715/1,945/2,167 by cc band) — confirm the current-year premium with your insurer
- **Fitness certificate** — Required for vehicles older than 15 years before renewal

<h2 id="how-to-pay">How to Pay — Online vs Offline</h2>

The FY 2083/84 gazette confirms all four payment channels are valid:

- Online banking via connectIPS and commercial bank portals
- Mobile wallets — eSewa, Khalti, IME Pay (wallet fee set by provider)
- Bank challan at Nepal Bank Ltd / Rastriya Banijya Bank authorized branches
- In-person at the district Transport Management Office (TMO)

<h3 id="online-steps">Online Payment Steps (Nagarik App / eSewa / Khalti)</h3>

1. Open Nagarik App → Government Payments → Vehicle Tax (or eSewa/Khalti → Government → Vehicle Tax)
2. Enter your vehicle registration (lot) number from the bluebook
3. The system auto-calculates tax by cc/age/fuel from the provincial schedule
4. Confirm amount and pay — save the payment receipt/voucher (screenshot + PDF)
5. Renew your third-party insurance if it expires before the new fiscal year

<h3 id="office-steps">Office Stamping (Mandatory)</h3>

1. Visit your district TMO (Ekantakuna, Gurjudhara in Valley) within a few days of online payment
2. Bring: original bluebook + printed/digital payment receipt + valid insurance + owner ID copy + previous receipt
3. Submit at the renewal counter — staff verify payment, insurance and tax calculation
4. Collect the renewed bluebook — check the new renewal stamp date and signature before leaving

> **10-minute job:** With all documents ready the counter visit is usually 10–15 minutes. Without valid insurance you will be turned away — renew insurance first.

<h2 id="penalties">Late Penalties &amp; Cancellation</h2>

- **First 30 days after expiry** — 5% of tax
- **Up to 45 days** — 10% of tax
- **Until fiscal-year end (Ashad)** — 20% of tax
- **Each year beyond (up to 5 years)** — 32% of tax per year
- **Renewal-charge penalty** — 100% of renewal fee (Rs 300→600, Rs 500→1,000) if late
- **After 5 consecutive years** — Registration may be cancelled — re-registration required

Example: a 150cc bike (Rs 5,000 tax) 40 days late pays ~Rs 500 penalty (10%) + Rs 300 renewal → Rs 5,800 plus insurance.

<h2 id="province-note">Province Variation</h2>

Rates above are Bagmati Province, where most vehicles are registered. Gandaki, Lumbini, Koshi and others use the same cc brackets but adjust each slab by ~2–10%. Always calculate via your province’s portal or ask the counter for “FY 2083/84 vehicle tax schedule.”

<h2 id="mistakes">Common Mistakes to Avoid</h2>

- Waiting until Shrawan — penalty jumps to 32% once the new fiscal year starts
- Paying online but never visiting for stamping — online voucher alone does not legalize the bluebook
- Renewing insurance after the tax payment — office will reject; renew insurance first
- Using model name cc (e.g., “Pulsar 150”) instead of actual displacement (149.5cc = ≤150cc bracket)
- Losing the previous receipt — get a duplicate at the TMO before queuing for renewal
- Assuming rates are federal — they are provincial and change each fiscal year with the gazette
- Riding with an expired bluebook — illegal and may lead to detention/fine at a checkpoint

<h2 id="checklist">Renewal Checklist</h2>

- Check expiry date stamped inside bluebook
- Confirm engine cc on the registration card
- Renew third-party insurance first
- Pay vehicle tax + renewal charge online (Nagarik/eSewa/Khalti/connectIPS) or prepare for counter payment
- Print payment receipt
- Visit TMO with: bluebook + receipt + insurance + ID copy + previous receipt
- Verify new stamp date/signature before leaving
- Save photo of renewed page + receipt for next year
